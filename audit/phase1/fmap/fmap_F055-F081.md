Pitfall recorded:
- The lab Python prints in cp1252. `p1_peek.py` `head`/`cat` crash on a BOM or other non-cp1252 text.
- Workarounds: `grep` with `^[^\ufeff]*…`, or `^[\x09-\x0d\x20-\x7e]+\Z` for pure-ASCII lines. For UTF-16 logs, allow `\x00` and strip it afterwards.
- A `|` or `(` inside ARGS is parsed by PowerShell and silently breaks the call.
