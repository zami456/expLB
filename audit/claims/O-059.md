## Robustness and caveats for the thesis

- **The "17 methods" equivalence claim is a median of medians, not a test.** The four actual
  dataset-bootstrap equivalence tests in `significance.json` all return
  `equivalent_within_margin = False` (they are equivalent only under the **row** bootstrap).
  §7.6's own wording — "the four equivalence tests have 90 % CIs from −0.038 to +0.033, so the
  two are equivalent within about ±0.04" — quietly widens the ±0.02 pre-registered margin to
  ±0.04. The §1.2 line "20 vs 22 is immaterial" inherits that.
- **The two medians are not like-for-like populations.** The raw group is 4 P1-transfer rows +
  6 Pass C LODO rows; the persona group is 8 P1-transfer rows + 5 zero-shot rows. Zero-shot
  persona rows carry the two largest gains (+0.234, +0.178). Dropping the zero-shot rows leaves
  8 persona rows with median ≈ +0.113 (unchanged) — so the headline survives, but the
  comparison still pools protocols the doc elsewhere warns are not comparable across rows
  (see claim O-086's note).
- No seed variation: every row is seed 42, one run.

## Verdict
