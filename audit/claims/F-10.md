# F-10 — "Score-3 filtering imposes an asymmetric sample imbalance"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
In the 36-role kd persona bank, keeping only judge-score-3 rollouts (`s3`) leaves
**116.8 / 89.0 / 73.3** rollouts per role in `knowledge_denial` / `sincere_ignorance` /
`plain_assertion`; keeping scores 2 and 3 (`s23`) leaves **146.8 / 150.0 / 149.0**. The
imbalance is **1.31×** against sincere and **1.59×** against assertion under s3, and
**0.98×** under s23. Only **16 of 1,500** new-group rollouts scored 0 or 1, so s3 discards
**~30%** that were merely partial. An earlier "395 vs 267 kept rollouts" is corrected to
**467 vs 267**.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:155-174`, citing `run-kd/scores/*.json` via
`kd/scripts/verify_s23.py`. The numbers were **print-only** — no result file holds them.

**One loose number.** "s3 discards **~30%** that were merely partial": the rollouts scoring
exactly 2 are **530 of 1,500 = 35.3%**. The total s3 discard is (1,500 − 954)/1,500 = 36.4%,
of which 530 are score-2 and 16 are score-0/1. So the honest figure is ~35%, not ~30%. The
argument is unaffected.

## The mechanism behind the imbalance, made explicit
The per-role histograms show it is driven by two roles, not spread evenly:

- `pc_deniable_expert` (78 of 150 at score 2) and `pc_amnesiac_witness`/`pc_stonewaller`/
  `pc_feigned_novice` (only 12–17 at score 2) — so even inside `knowledge_denial` the keep
  rate ranges 72–137 of 150;
- every honest role loses 40–84 rollouts to score 2 (`pc_candid_witness` 84, `pc_scrupulous_
  witness` 76, `pc_plain_dealer` 73, `pc_forthright_expert` 70, `pc_calibrated_reporter` 67,
  `pc_honest_novice` 40).

That is exactly F-10's stated reason — "answer plainly without softening" sits close to default
assistant behaviour, so the honest roles lose their most default-like rollouts — but it is an
interpretation of the counts, not itself measured here. The measurement that *does* support it
is F-11/F-19: the surviving honest rollouts sit further from the Assistant anchor.

One minor bookkeeping point: `kd/personas/kd_role_groups.json` declares **36** roles but
`run-kd/scores/` holds **35** score files. The missing one is the `default` anchor, which is
not judged; none of the three contrasted groups is affected.
