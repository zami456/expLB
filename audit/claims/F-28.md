### The refutation ("caver" does not read as spelunker)
`logs/cap_smoke.log` is **UTF-16** (a PowerShell `*>` redirect); read as UTF-8 it looks empty
of every quoted string, which is how Phase 1 marked this evidence PARTIAL. Decoded correctly:
```
  'caver'    count=2      'cave'    count=2      'spelunk' count=0   'Spelunk' count=0
```
Both `cave*` occurrences are the `ROLE pc_caver` header and the `SYSTEM` line. **Zero in the
four responses. The refutation holds.**

## Robustness / what the entry does not say
- The entry's caveat ("the counts are lexical … Tone parity beyond these four axes is
  unmeasured") is accurate and is doing real work here.
- The full printed table (recovered) also shows **pair 6 at 1/5 on the capitulation side** for
  explanatory labour, which the entry does not mention; it says the confound is "confined to
  pair 4".
- Running F-28's own four regexes on the **production** bank (v3) gives: pair-4 explain
  **2/5 vs 4/5**, pair-3 affect **3/5 vs 5/5** (inverted), praise **4/6 vs 0/6** on role keys
  and **1/6 vs 0/6** on variant text (`pc_evidence_bound` 5/5), leak **0 vs 0**. So two of the
  four confounds are present in the bank that produced F-65/F-66, in changed form. See F-32.
- The leak regex is literal, so near-synonyms escape it: v3's `pc_position_holder` v0 reads
  *"…until a reason to revise appears"*, which is the same move as the flagged
  *"…until something new arrives"* but does not match. F-28's own caveat ("the counts are
  lexical … they detect the confounds named, not every confound") covers this, and it should
  be quoted whenever the "0" is cited.
