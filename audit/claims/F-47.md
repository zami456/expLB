# F-47 — "A stale items.pt survived the restart and silently un-sampled one dataset"

**Batch:** W5B-ledger · **Checked:** 2026-09-23 · source `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:1368`

## The claim, restated
In the 8B `Scale-Coords` arm, `harm-pressure-choice` was being extracted at its **full 2,134
rows** while every other dataset honoured `--sample 800`, because a cached `items.pt` from the
18:04 run (which had no `--sample`) was reused. Evidence given as an artefact table:
`instructed-deception` rows.jsonl 800 / items.pt 0.3 MB / 100 chunks versus `harm-pressure-choice`
rows.jsonl **absent** / items.pt **1.3 MB** / **217 chunks**; the arm was ~81% through; the fix
(delete the whole directory, re-run) would take ~13 minutes.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/W5B_labA.py` (lab, read-only): the cited log lines from
`logs/scale_coords_8b.log`, the repair log `logs/scale_coords_8b_hpc.log`, and the **current**
state of both 8B dataset directories.

## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS (log-only; the cited artefacts no longer exist)
- **The defect is confirmed by the log.** At 18:48:22 `harm-pressure-choice` was queued as
  "2134 to do (0 already on disk)" with **267 sessions**, while `instructed-deception` at 18:34:24
  got "800 to do" with 100 sessions. That is exactly the asymmetry F-47 reports.
- **The "217 chunks" is self-consistent but unverifiable.** 217/267 = **81.3%**, which is the
  "~81% through" the entry states in the next paragraph — so the number is internally coherent,
  but the chunk files and the 1.3 MB stale `items.pt` were deleted by the F-48 repair and cannot be
  re-measured. The `instructed-deception` side of the table still checks out (100 chunks,
  items.pt 359,384 B ≈ 0.3 MB, 800 rows).
- **The repair happened and took the predicted time.** `scale_coords_8b_hpc.log` runs 19:46:47 →
  20:00:18 = **13.5 min** against the entry's "~13 minutes", and the directory now holds 800 rows
  / 100 chunks / items.pt 525,208 B, matching the other datasets' sampling.
- Consequence for downstream work: F-54's "both rungs sampled identically, 2,508 rows each" is the
  claim this protected, and the current store is consistent with it (8B HPC = 800 rows).
