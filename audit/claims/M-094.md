## Verdict — HOLDS
Under the **uncentered** convention (plain dot product with the sign-pinned PC1) the four numbers
are **−2.7491 / −3.5952 / +1.4917 / +0.0732** against the note's −2.75 / −3.60 / +1.49 / +0.07 —
exact to two decimals. `PC1 var = 0.467` and `PC2 var = 0.188` also match the note's 46.7% / 18.8%.

**The caveat that matters for anyone reusing these numbers.** Re-centring on the 26-role mean shifts
**all four by the same +1.65** (to −1.10 / −1.95 / +3.14 / +1.72). The absolute values are therefore
convention-dependent; only the **split** is not. Stated invariantly: the two deception-question
groups average **−3.14** and the two sycophancy-question groups **+0.78**, a gap of **3.93** on PC1,
while the behaviour contrast *within* each family (deception vs honesty, +0.85; sycophancy vs
anti-sycophancy, +1.42) is much smaller. That is the claim, and it is solid.
