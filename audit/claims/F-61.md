# F-61 — the 36-vs-26 bank comparison is confounded THREE ways

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:1905` (batch W5C-ledger).

## The claim, in one sentence
The kd bank (36 personas, 30 questions, 5 variants, 150 rollouts/role, 5,400 total) scores
`pc:unified − raw` **+0.076** at L40 on the 11-dataset grid where the paper16 bank (26 personas,
**10** questions, 5 variants, **50** rollouts/role, 1,400 total) scores **+0.006** — a twelvefold
difference — but persona count, question set and rollouts-per-role all changed at once, the
question sets being "*different questions*, not a superset", and `unified` does not even include
the 10 new kd roles, so none of the gain can be attributed to persona design.

## What I did
1. Counted role files, rollouts, distinct question texts and `prompt_index` values in
   `run-paper16/responses/*.jsonl` and `run-kd/responses/*.jsonl` on the lab PC, per role and in
   total (`W5C_labB.py`, `W5C_labC.py`, read-only).
2. Compared the two question-text sets exactly.
3. Recomputed `pc:unified` and `raw` off-diagonal means (paper probe, mean pooling, L40, seed
   42) from the matrices, joining the controls from the **root** store as the code requires
   (`F-61_local.py` on the git copies; `W5C_labB.py` on the lab copies — identical results).
4. Searched every matrix under `run-crossgen/**` on both the lab and the laptop for the reading
   the session console printed (raw 0.6800 over **109** pairs).
5. Read `6_axes.py:188–195` for what goes into `unified`.

## Verdict: **PARTLY HOLDS** — the conclusion stands and is strengthened; the table and the
"+0.006" do not

The entry's **conclusion** — that the two banks differ in personas, questions and sample size
at once, so the gain cannot be attributed to persona design, and "the 36-persona bank is better"
is unsupported — is correct, and my measurements make it *more* true, not less. But:

1. **The composition table is wrong in two cells.**
   - paper16 questions are **22**, not 10. The bank uses **two** question sets: a 10-question
     set for the 16 deception/honesty roles (incl. `default`) and a **12**-question set for the
     10 sycophancy/anti-sycophancy roles. (This matches the paper's own §4: sycophancy uses
     12 questions.)
   - rollouts/role is **50 for 16 roles and 60 for 10** (mean 53.8), not 50 for all. This is
     why the entry's own arithmetic does not close: 26 × 50 = 1,300, but the total is 1,400.
   - The "50 / 10 questions / 5 variants" line was measured on **one** role file
     (`default.jsonl`) and generalised — the session transcript shows exactly that command.
   - kd's row is exact: 36 roles × 30 questions × 5 `prompt_index` values = 150/role = 5,400.
2. **Confound (2) "*different questions*, not a superset" is FALSE.** **20 of the 22** paper16
   question texts appear verbatim among the kd 30. Only 2 paper16 questions are absent, and kd
   adds 10. So kd is very nearly a superset, which weakens that confound and strengthens
   confound (3) (sample size) — the direction the entry already argues for.
3. **"+0.006" does not reproduce.** The like-for-like value — paper16 `pc:unified` minus `raw`,
   off-diagonal, 11 datasets, paper probe, mean pooling, L40, seed 42, k = 5 — is **+0.0093**
   (110 pairs). `+0.0064` is `pc:unified − **datapca**` at the same setting (and +0.0063 at
   k = 2), so the ledger's "+0.006" is most likely the matched-control margin quoted as the raw
   margin. The console reading it came from (raw = 0.6800 over **109** pairs) exists in **no**
   matrix under `run-crossgen` on either the lab PC or the laptop, so it cannot be reproduced
   at all. `last` pooling gives −0.0259, so it is not a pooling mix-up either.
   → the "**twelvefold**" ratio is really **8.1×** (0.0757 / 0.0093), using both banks' numbers
   from the same root raw baseline over the same 110 pairs.
4. **kd's +0.076 is exact**: +0.0757 ✓, and it must be joined to the **root** store for `raw`
   and `datapca` — the kd bank matrix carries only `axes`, `axes_resid` and its `pc:*` features.
5. **"`unified` does not include the new groups" is VERIFIED** at `6_axes.py:188–195`: it is
   built from `deception + sycophancy` vs `honesty + anti_sycophancy` only.
6. The entry's caveat (F-31's dataset-level bootstrap CI [−0.006, +0.173], q = 0.499) is
   unchanged by any of this; it is checked under F-31, not here.
