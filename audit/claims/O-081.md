## Robustness
- The **dataset set matters**: this is the **10-labelled-dataset** matrix, i.e. with
  `sycophancy_mt`. 90 ordered off-diagonal cells (10 × 9). The 11th mapped dataset (`ai_audit`) is
  in the semantics map but not in the matrix.
- The gap is direction-consistent for every feature, but the permutation p only crosses 0.05 for
  `raw/mms` (0.043), `pc:deception/mms` (0.027) and `pc:deception/lr_reg10` (0.042); `axes/mms`
  gives 0.103 and `raw/lr_reg10` 0.286. So "looks like clustering by construct" is a one-of-six
  reading at the conventional threshold — and the document's own verdict immediately argues it away.
- The random-feature control gives a within/across gap of 0.044–0.047 at p ≈ 0.21, so a positive gap
  is not automatic.
