# O-070 — best against best: axes@44 beats raw@32 by +0.014 (mean) and raw@28 by +0.026 (last)

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:587-588` (§7.2, "What holds on these
nine datasets").

**Claim as stated:** "Best against best: axes@44 beats raw@32 by **+0.014** (mean), and axes@44
beats raw@28 by **+0.026** (last)."

**Restated:** mean off-diagonal P1 transfer AUROC, 9 labelled datasets (72 pairs), Pass B, `mms`
probe, paper16 fp32 bank, seed 42. Take each method at its own best layer and subtract.

layer sweeps, then cross-checks against the bootstrap file.

## Level 1 / Level 2

Level 1: `significance.json` states +0.014161 and +0.025898 → +0.014 and +0.026. **Match.**
Level 2: recomputed independently from `layer_sweep_passB_mms.json` and
`layer_sweep_passB_last.json` by taking the argmax over all 21 stored layers — the peak layers
(raw L32 mean / L28 last, axes L44 under both poolings) and both differences reproduce to 6 dp.
§7.2's claim "the persona axes peak at 44 under both poolings" is therefore also confirmed.

## The number is real; the sentence around it is the problem

1. **Neither gap is significant across datasets.** The same file that stores them records
   dataset_p 0.8612 and 0.598, BH q 0.86 and 0.67. §7.2 says this three bullets later
   ("Not significant across datasets"), but the "Best against best" bullet is under the heading
   "**What holds** on these nine datasets".
2. **A best-vs-best difference is a maximum over 21 layers on both sides**, taken on one seed,
   with no selection correction. The doc's own §8 does not offer one.
3. **It does not survive a different dataset set or a different store.** Robustness sweep over
   `audit/phase2/layer_sensitivity.csv` (the crossgen family, dataset_set = 'all', i.e. 10 or 11
   datasets, layers ≥ 20, 126 store × bank × pooling × seed × probe configurations):

```
O-070  axes minus raw, best-vs-best, across stores (dataset_set='all')
  best-vs-best axes-raw over 126 configurations: median -0.0314, positive 28/126
  e.g. <root> paper16 mean seed42 mms 10ds: raw L32 0.7153 vs axes L44 0.6709 -> -0.0444
       <root> paper16 last seed42 mms 10ds: raw L28 0.6932 vs axes L44 0.6519 -> -0.0413
       passB_fine      last seed42 mms 10ds: raw L37 0.6756 vs axes L44 0.6515 -> -0.0241
       passC           last seed42 mms 10ds: raw L28 0.6856 vs axes L44 0.6392 -> -0.0464
```
   In 98 of 126 configurations the best-vs-best gap is **negative**. The +0.014 / +0.026 pair
   is specific to the 9-dataset Pass B run-coords store. (Note these stores are not a like-for-like
   replication of the 9-dataset number — they use 10 or 11 datasets — so this is a robustness
   signal, not a contradiction of the quoted value.)

## Verdict

**HOLDS.** Both numbers reproduce exactly at Level 1 and Level 2, including the peak layers.

*Confidence:* HIGH for the arithmetic.
*Importance:* HIGH — this is the paper's headline persona-vs-raw advantage. For the reframed
thesis the honest statement is: "+0.014 / +0.026 at each method's own best layer, on one seed,
on one 9-dataset set; dataset-bootstrap q 0.86 and 0.67; the same comparison is negative in 98
of 126 other configurations."
