## Caveats for the reframed thesis

1. **"Random erasures ~0.000" rests on only five random directions**, and the file stores their
   mean, not a distribution. This is the table's weakest control.
2. **Three of six rows are BH-significant costs.** The §1.2 one-liner ("costs only 0.005–0.018")
   and the §7.4 verdict ("small but real") are both fair, but the table's rightmost column is the
   one that says the assistant direction is *not* free.
3. §7.4's verdict says the erasure costs "1–3 % of transfer AUROC"; the measured range is
   **0.80–2.64 %**, with two rows below 1 %. Small, but it is a printed number that does not
   match its file.
4. The erased direction is bank-local, cos 0.30–0.37 with the published Assistant Axis (doc's own
   §10 caveat; independently confirmed in claim O-057). "The Assistant Axis" has not been erased.
