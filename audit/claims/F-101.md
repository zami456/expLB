# F-101 — the 70B rung under `last` pooling: F-100's "persona buys nothing over raw" is pooling-dependent

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:4674` (2026-09-22).

## The claim in one sentence
For the 70B rung only (21 stored layers, k=3, `probe=paper`, seed 42, paper's five datasets),
switching from **mean** to **last**-token pooling raises the persona advantage over raw activations
from **+0.0070 (positive at 11/21 layers)** to **+0.0333 (19/21)**, raises the matched-control margin
from +0.0791 to +0.0945, leaves the fixed-feature `axes − datapca` unchanged (+0.0383 vs +0.0366),
and turns L40 from a mid-pack layer (rank 13/21, +0.0794) into the **second-worst of 21**
(rank 19/21, +0.0433).

## The bank problem (RF-16 / F-110) — and what it does to this entry
F-101's source line names `run-kd/axes_vectors_s3.pt` as the bank — so, unlike F-100, F-101 *states*
the kd bank. But it compares that 70B rung against F-100's paper16-bank 3B/8B numbers and inherits
F-100's framing. Recomputing the same quantities on the audit's like-for-like paper16 re-run
(`run-crossgen/scale_70b_paper16fp32`, 42 matrices):

| 70B, 21 layers | kd bank (as F-101 ran it) | paper16 fp32 (like-for-like) |
|---|---:|---:|
| best − raw, mean | +0.0070 (11/21) | **−0.0171 (8/21)** |
| best − raw, last | +0.0333 (19/21) | **+0.0069 (12/21)** |
| best − dPCA, mean | +0.0791 | **+0.0550** |
| best − dPCA, last | +0.0945 | **+0.0681** |
| L40 rank on best − dPCA, mean / last | 13/21 / **19/21** | 6/21 / **14/21** |

**Two consequences.**
1. The entry's central contrast **survives**: `last` pooling still gives a larger margin over raw than
   `mean` does (+0.0069 vs −0.0171 like-for-like), and still a larger matched-control margin
   (+0.0681 vs +0.0550). The direction is the same; the magnitudes are roughly halved.
2. The entry's second headline — **"L40 is the second-worst layer of 21 under `last`"** — does **not**
   survive the bank fix: like-for-like L40 ranks **14 of 21**, mid-pack. "Quoting L40 understates the
   margin by more than a factor of two" (+0.0433 vs +0.0945) becomes a factor of ~1.6
   (+0.0434 vs +0.0681). The rhetorical point ("which way a single layer biases a result depends on
   the pooling") still holds, but the specific 19/21 ranking is a kd-bank artifact.

## Robustness
One seed (42); 20 off-diagonal pairs per cell; "best" is a post-hoc per-layer max over `axes` and
`pc:deception`, as the entry's caveat (2) says. The 3B/8B rungs were not swept under `last` at the
time — F-102 then found their `last` matrices already on disk.

## Verdict
