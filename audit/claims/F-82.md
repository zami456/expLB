## Verdict — HOLDS
Every published figure reproduces to the last digit: 21/21 and 21/21, longest run 21 and 21,
**+0.1542 vs +0.1538** (difference **0.0004**, as stated), **[+0.0913, +0.2113] vs
[+0.0907, +0.2118]**, P(≤0) = 0.0000 both, argmax band **[L28, L60]** both, argmax median
**L36 (s23) vs L32 (all)**. The caveat's "lands on L40 in 3.1% of resamples" is reproduced for
`cap_all` (the filtered bank gives 3.9%).

Robustness / scope, recorded because the entry's headline could be read more broadly than it is:
- the margin is measured against **`datapca@2`** (the matched control), not against raw; the
  feature−raw column is smaller and peaks differently (+0.139 at L32, +0.112 at L40);
- `n_src = 10`, i.e. the bootstrap resamples **ten** source datasets — the whole interval rests on
  ten units, which is why the argmax band spans L28–L60;
- the target is the single `sycophancy_mt` column, which is the F-67 locality caveat.

**Confidence:** HIGH. Deterministic recomputation from the stored matrices with the script's own
seed; independent of any bootstrap luck.
