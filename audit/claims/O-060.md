# O-060 — Q8: zero-shot top-10 % beats mean pooling; trained pooled beats per-token at every layer

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:53` (§1.2), backed by §7.8
(lines 764–825).

**Claim as stated:** "Zero-shot: per-token top-10 % scoring beats mean pooling. Trained: pooled
probes beat per-token probes at every layer. | Trained half significant at layers 20 and 28 only."

**Restated:** (a) zero-shot — projecting each response token onto the paper16 deception axis and
taking the mean of the top decile of token scores gives a higher pooled-corpus AUROC than taking
the mean of all token scores, at each of the 6 Pass C layers (20, 22, 28, 40, 44, 48);
(b) trained — under Pass C leave-one-dataset-out over 9 held-out datasets, a probe trained on the
pooled (mean) activation beats every probe trained on individual tokens, at all 6 layers;
(c) that trained advantage clears BH correction over datasets only at layers 20 and 28.

## Level 1 / Level 2

- **(a)** top-10 % > mean at 6/6 layers, gaps +0.0023 to +0.1027. The doc's §7.8 zero-shot table
  reproduces cell by cell (0.695/0.741/0.681/0.775/0.798/0.650 at L20, etc.), and its
  "best against best: 0.807 (L28) vs 0.765 (L40), +0.042" is 0.8067 − 0.7652 = +0.0415. **Match.**
- **(b)** pooled beats the *best* token probe at 6/6 layers, gap +0.0276 … +0.0604. §7.8's "by
  0.027–0.060" is exactly that range. **Match.** A stronger form also holds: `pool_mean_mms`
  beats *every* one of the 8–12 token-probe rows at every layer (worst-case gap +0.0276).
- **(c)** significance: 4 of the 8 stored tests clear BH — both at L20 and both at L28
  (q 0.00095–0.02375); none at L40/L44. §7.8's "pooled wins 8–9 of 9 folds, q ≤ 0.024" matches
  (folds 8/9, 9/9, 8/9, 9/9; max q 0.02375). **Match.**

Level 2 was a re-derivation of "best pooled" and "best token" from the 106 raw `results`
records rather than a read of the `summary` block — the two agree.

## Caveats that matter for a reframed thesis

1. **"Significant at layers 20 and 28 only" is only tested at four of the six layers.**
   Layers 22 and 48 were never entered into a significance test. The §1.2 wording implies a
   6-layer sweep; the §7.8 wording ("not at 40 or 44") is correct.
2. **The significance tests compare pooled-mean-mms against `tok_lr` only** — but the *best*
   token probe at L20/L22/L28 is `tok_mms`, which was never tested. At L20 the pooled-vs-best-token
   gap is +0.0604 and is untested; the tested gap is +0.072 against a weaker token probe.
3. The two halves point opposite ways, which the doc states plainly ("quote both"); the §1.2
   one-liner keeps that, correctly.
4. The Pass C Alpaca control is 200 rows, so the TPR@1%FPR column in the same table is set by
   2 rows — the doc says so. It is not part of this claim but sits in the same table.
