**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:408` (status in doc: *verified*). Batch P1-hpkr.

## The claim, stated precisely
1. `harm-pressure-knowledge-report` comes from **Liars' Bench** and is **not used by
   Mahadik & Skapars at all**.
2. `harm-pressure-kr-matched` is **ours**, constructed **2026-09-19**.
3. The paper's **Table 1 lists HarmPressureChoice**, and even that **never appears in any
   figure** — **Figure 7's own title reads "[HPC excluded]"**.
4. The paper's **figures use MASK**, which has **no row in Table 1**.
5. That inconsistency is flagged in `docs/papers/persona-coordinates-notes.md` §8.1.
6. Therefore the paper had no opportunity to detect the wording confound: it never evaluated
   on a dataset where the lie *is* a denial.

## What I did

the PDF itself: `audit/scripts/claims/F-23.py` extracts the text of all 14 pages of
`papers/Do Linear Probes Generalize Better in Persona Coordinates.pdf` with pypdf and
counts every dataset name, dumps Table 1, dumps every figure caption, and greps for
"excluded"/"HPC". Figure axis labels are embedded text in this PDF (AILiar and Mask are
extracted from figure pages), so a name absent from the extraction is absent from the
figures. Then I checked the claimed construction date of the slice against the lab log.

## One detail the entry could sharpen
F-23 says "even that never appears in any figure". Strictly, HPC appears in **two** figures —
as the words "[HPC excluded]" in the titles of Figure 7 and Figure 8. What never appears is
any HPC *result*. The entry's point stands; the phrasing is loose.

Also worth noting for the write-up: the exclusion is not hidden. The paper's authors put
"[HPC excluded]" in their own figure titles, so this is a documented omission on their side,
not something we caught them at. What is undocumented is **MASK's** use without a Table 1 row.
