## A definitional caveat worth carrying into the paper
"Significantly inverted" here means *the per-cell bootstrap upper bound is below 0.5*, i.e. a
**row-level** bootstrap over test rows, not a dataset-level one. Given RF-13/F-74's repeated
finding that row-level intervals are far too tight for a generalisation claim, the "16 of 23"
figure should be quoted as "16 of 23 with a row-level interval entirely below chance", not as
a statement about datasets.
