## Verdict: PARTLY HOLDS
**Everything F-73 measured reproduces exactly**: all four AUROCs to 4 decimals, every cell of
the 11-row gap table to the precision printed, "longer in 9 of 11", "6 of 8", and the median
gap 3.689 → 3.7. The two figures it corrects (`mask` 283.2 vs 176.4; `sandbagging_v2` 529.4
vs 340.4) are exactly what the store says.

Two statements inside the entry do **not** survive:

1. **"Our stated 0.553 vs 0.665 is confirmed to three decimals."** Measured: **0.5555 and
   0.6623**, i.e. 0.556 / 0.662 to three decimals. The old figures differ by 0.0025 and
   0.0027 — agreement is to two decimals, not three. F-73's own closing paragraph gets this
   right ("0.556 off-diagonal against raw's 0.662"), so the entry contradicts itself.
2. **"On `harm-pressure-choice` the gap is exactly zero — single-token responses."** Measured
   +0.0035 (1.0075 vs 1.0040): a few responses exceed one token. Immaterial, but "exactly
   zero … by construction" is not what the file says.

**A related stale number outside F-73, worth the owner's attention.** Five documents still
say the positive class is longer in **9 of 10** datasets
(`docs/ONBOARDING.md:78`, `docs/NOVELTY_CHECK_PROMPT.md:110`, `kd/docs/DESIGN.md:176,202`,
`docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:497,1105`, memory note
`response-length-transfers-on-its-own`). On this store the count is **8 of 10** on the
ten-dataset `block9` set and **9 of 11** with `sycophancy_mt` added. "9 of 10" matches
neither set.
