## Two defects found

**1. A range attached to the wrong feature.** F-100 writes: "`axes` **loses to raw at every layer from
44 to 68** (−0.004 to −0.029) while `pc:deception` holds up". Measured on the same store:

```
70B kd, mean, axes - raw:  L44 -0.0221  L48 -0.0407  L52 -0.0508  L56 -0.0800  L60 -0.0868  L64 -0.0998  L68 -0.1059
70B kd, mean, best - raw:  L44 -0.0041  L48 -0.0171  L52 -0.0199  L56 -0.0291  L60 -0.0238  L64 -0.0202  L68 -0.0071
   (at these layers "best" is pc:deception throughout)
```

The quoted range **−0.004 to −0.029 is the `pc:deception − raw` range**, not `axes − raw`
(which is −0.022 to −0.106, four times larger). And "`pc:deception` holds up" is not right either:
pc:deception **also loses to raw at every one of those layers**, just by less. The qualitative point
(the "best" column is carried by pc:deception at depth) stands; the numbers are mislabelled.

**2. The bank error, already corrected as F-110.** F-100's source line says the sweep used "the same
bank and datasets as the existing L40 cell" (`run-kd/axes_vectors_s3.pt`, `bank_name=paper16_70b`).
Per RF-16 the L40 cell is the **paper16** bank and the rest of the sweep is the **36-persona kd**
bank, so the 70B rung is not like-for-like with the paper16 3B/8B rungs. Confirmed here from the file
fields and from the audit's re-run: `scale_70b/mean_L40` records `bank=axes_vectors_s3.pt`,
mtime 2026-09-20 23:42, and agrees with `scale_70b_paper16fp32/mean_L40` to 0.0001. Like-for-like the
70B rung reads **+0.0541 vs dPCA and −0.0131 vs raw** (19 layers), so:
- "+0.120 / +0.103 / +0.075" becomes **+0.120 / +0.103 / +0.054**;
- "against raw the 70B persona advantage is essentially nil (+0.0079)" becomes **negative (−0.0131)**.

## Robustness

- One seed (42), 20 off-diagonal pairs per cell; "best" is a post-hoc per-layer max over two features,
