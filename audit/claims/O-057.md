## Two precision findings

1. **The hf275 column is measured against the PUBLISHED Assistant Axis, not against the
   bank's own 100-role assistant direction.** Against the 100-role bank's own direction the
   column reads −0.669 / −0.651 / −0.577 / −0.479 — 0.02–0.04 away from the printed numbers.
   The printed numbers reproduce to 4 dp only against `published_assistant_axis.pt` (or,
   equivalently to ±0.002, against an assistant direction built over all 275 roles, which has
   cos 0.99995 with the published axis). So the doc's sentence "Against **each bank's own**
   assistant direction, the paper's personas halve the contamination" is loose for the hf275
   column — though the substantive conclusion is untouched, because the two readings differ
   by ≤ 0.04.
2. The doc's "Verified: the hf275 column, recomputed from the cached published vectors (exact
   match)" is true, and this audit reproduces it — but only under that reading.

## Robustness

The relation holds at every stored layer (20, 22, 40, 60), not just L40; paper16 is cleaner
than hf275 by 0.18–0.32 everywhere. RF-15 is not a threat: the old-vs-fp32 rebuild moves
cos(dec, asst) by at most 0.00065 over all 80 layers (see O-064).

raw vectors. The third (−0.66, own100) rests on a lab-only vector store and could not be checked
