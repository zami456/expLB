# F-37 — persona geometry across three model sizes: size-dependent, NOT monotone

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:941` (2026-09-20).

## The claim in one sentence
With the same paper16 bank at each rung's midpoint layer (3B L14/28, 8B L16/32, 70B L40/80),
cos(PC1 of the all-roles PCA, the assistant axis) is **+0.435 / +0.732 / +0.623** — non-monotone,
peaking at 8B — while the per-behaviour contrast alignments and the deception-basis PC1 variance
*are* monotone (sycophancy 0.948 / 0.983 / 0.993; deception PC1 var 30.2% / 36.0% / 43.0%).

## Two further problems found

1. **The threshold verdicts are UNDERSPECIFIED and only work under one reading.** F-37 says the
   ">0.60 all layers" test is passed by the 8B, but its own table gives the 8B's **min across layers
   as +0.514**. The sentence is self-consistent only if "all layers" is applied to the *median*
   (8B 0.701 ✓, 70B 0.618 ✓, 3B 0.532 ✗). Under the literal "at every layer" reading, **all three
   rungs fail**. The entry does not say which it means.
2. **The caveat about the 70B column is contradicted.** F-37 says "the 70B column is paper16-shared;
   per F-18 it differs from plain paper16 **only in the unified basis**". Measured at L40: the plain
   `run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3.pt` gives |cos(PC1_all, assistant)| **0.8162** and PC1 variance
   **46.7%**, against the shared file's 0.5838 and 36.8%. The all-roles basis differs substantially
   too, not only the unified one (0.3890 vs 0.8957). Whatever F-18 established, this caveat as
   written is not supported.

## Verdict
**PARTLY HOLDS.** The entire table matches its source logs exactly, the three columns are like-for-like
(all `vectors_all`), and the 70B column recomputes exactly from the surviving `.pt`. But the 3B and 8B
columns are **log-only** — the axes files they were computed from were overwritten by the later
score-filtered extraction — and two supporting statements fail: the ">0.60 all layers" verdicts hold
only under a median reading the entry never states, and the "differs only in the unified basis"
caveat is contradicted by the files.
Confidence **HIGH** on the Level-1 match and on the 70B recompute; **MEDIUM** on the 3B/8B values,
which rest on a log rather than a file.
Importance **MEDIUM** — it is the geometry half of the scale story (figure `fig4_geometry_by_scale`),
and its qualitative conclusion is robust to the bank choice, but its per-number precision is not
reproducible at two of three rungs.
