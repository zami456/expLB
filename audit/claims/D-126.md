## Robustness — what "best" can mean with three layers

- Only **three** layers exist on his branch (20/40/44); there is no sweep, so "layer 40 is best"
  is a best-of-three statement.
- The owner's independent 21-layer sweep on her eleven datasets (F-99, verified in batch P3,
  zero-shot mean pooling) puts the extended contrast's maximum at **L36 (0.8018)**, just above
  **L40 (0.7996)**, and the 8v7 contrast's maximum at **L36 (0.7419)** above **L40 (0.7325)**.
  Different dataset set and different bank build, so it is not a refutation — but it says the
  peak is a plateau around L32–L40, not a point at 40.
- Her ledger also carries **F-91** ("Layer 40 is not the peak. L32 is, for both raw and persona
  features, by ~0.02") — **ledger-stated, not yet claim-checked in this audit**, flagged
  DOC-ONLY.
- The base probe peaks at a *different* layer (L20) on balanced accuracy, which the draft states
  separately (D-127, batch P8).
