## Robustness the entry already states, and one it does not
Stated: one seed; L0 negative under `last`; the share statistic unstable by construction. Confirmed —
the share exceeds 100% at L60/L64/L68 under mean (because `ext_axis1 − raw` there is *smaller* than
`ext_axis1 − axis1`) and is −155.9% at L0 under `last`. That is a stronger instability than
"62.9% vs 50.0%" conveys, and it reinforces the entry's own instruction to quote the raw difference
rather than the share.
Not stated: the difference is one bank (`collab`) and one dataset set (the eleven). Both are inherent
to the question.
