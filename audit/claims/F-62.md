## Verdict — HOLDS
All eight numbers and the baseline reproduce exactly to four decimal places with independently
written code, and the "largest gap 0.005" summary is right. Two things worth recording:

1. **The "no difference" result is a one-cell result.** The entry's own caveat says one layer, one
   seed, one pooling; measured across the other stored layers, poolings and probes the largest gap
   grows to **0.0301** (`last`, L20, `pc:knowledge_denial`: −0.0432 vs −0.0734) — six times the L40
   figure, though still far inside the ±0.17 dataset-level CI the entry appeals to. The conclusion
   survives; the phrase "NO measurable difference" is strongest exactly at the layer that was reported.
2. **The figure count is slightly off today.** s3 = 4 (exact). s23 = 4 under `blocks`/`matched` plus
   **8** under `passA/{blocks,matched,per_source,per_target}` (a `paper` and an `mms` version each),
   so 12, not 8. The `passA` figures entered git at `3dfdb67` (2026-09-21 11:07), i.e. **after** F-62
   was written, so "four more" was plausibly right on the day. No number depends on this.

**Confidence HIGH** (recomputed from files that are identical in git, on the lab PC and on Drive).

correctly at the layer reported.
