# O-068 — regrouping the HF roles by disclosure stance moves mean |cos| only 0.655 → 0.585

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:547-549` (§7.1, supporting findings).

**Claim as stated:** "**Relabelling the library does not fix it.** Regrouping the cached HF roles
by disclosure stance (8 concealment vs 14 disclosure roles) moves mean |cos| only from 0.655 to
0.585, about 11 %. The library's roles are occupations, so they are the wrong material."

**Restated:** build the deception contrast axis two ways from the same cached HF role vectors —
(a) "ours", the project's own 12-deceptive vs 12-honest grouping, and (b) "disclosure", a
regrouping into 8 concealment vs 14 disclosure roles — and compare |cos(deception, assistant)|
averaged over the four stored layers 20 / 22 / 40 / 60.

## What I did

`audit/scripts/claims/W6A_geom.py` reads `run-coords/regroup_hf.json` (1,770 B) and recomputes
the two means and the percentage change.

```
O-068  regrouping the cached HF roles by disclosure stance
n conceal = 8  n disclose = 14  (n_disclosure field in results: [8, 14] )
  L20  ours -0.7067   disclosure -0.6493
  L22  ours -0.6792   disclosure -0.6162
  L40  ours -0.6544   disclosure -0.5764
  L60  ours -0.5783   disclosure -0.4990
mean |cos| ours = 0.6547   disclosure = 0.5852   reduction = 10.60%  (layers [20, 22, 40, 60])
```

The role lists in the file:
`conceal` = spy, smuggler, rogue, saboteur, fixer, criminal, hacker, genie (8);
`disclose` = auditor, journalist, reporter, witness, observer, archivist, validator, examiner,
detective, judge, secretary, jester, prophet, realist (14);
`ours_dec` / `ours_hon` are 12 and 12.

## One thing a reader will trip over

The `ours` baseline here (0.655 mean, −0.654 at L40) is a **12-vs-12** contrast, which is *not*
the 22-vs-22 contrast that produces §7.1's hf275 column (−0.610 at L40). Three different
groupings of the same HF library appear within eight lines of §7.1 — 22v22 (the table),
12v12 (this bullet's baseline) and 8v14 (this bullet's alternative) — and the text does not
distinguish them. The comparison inside the bullet is internally consistent (both arms are
built the same way from the same vectors), so the claim stands; but the "0.655" cannot be
reconciled with the "−0.610" in the table above it without knowing this.

## Robustness

Only four layers are stored (20, 22, 40, 60), one grouping per arm, no bootstrap. The direction
of the effect is the same at all four layers (disclosure is cleaner by 0.057–0.079 everywhere),
so the sign of the conclusion is not layer-dependent, but "about 11 %" is a four-point mean.
