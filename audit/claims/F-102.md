## The claim in one sentence
Repeating the three-rung comparison (paper's five datasets, k=3, `probe=paper`, seed 42, all stored
layers: 7 / 8 / 21) under **last**-token pooling instead of mean: the matched-control margin becomes
**+0.0745 / +0.0090 / +0.0945** — non-monotone, with the 70B highest — and the margin over raw
**reverses sign with scale**, from **−0.0178 (2/7) / −0.0742 (0/8) / +0.0333 (19/21)** against
mean pooling's +0.0328 / +0.0089 / +0.0070; and (amendment) the obvious truncation explanation is
refuted because **no row in either small rung hits the 1024-token cap**.

## The bank problem (RF-16 / F-110) and what it does to this entry
The 70B column of both F-102 tables is the **kd-bank** sweep. On the audit's like-for-like paper16
re-run (`run-crossgen/scale_70b_paper16fp32`, 21 layers):

| matched control (best − dPCA) | 3B | 8B | 70B kd | 70B paper16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mean | +0.1197 | +0.1027 | +0.0791 | **+0.0550** |
| last | +0.0745 | +0.0090 | **+0.0945** | **+0.0681** |

| vs raw (best − raw) | 3B | 8B | 70B kd | 70B paper16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mean | +0.0328 | +0.0089 | +0.0070 | **−0.0171** |
| last | −0.0178 | −0.0742 | **+0.0333** | **+0.0069** |

- **"Under `last` the 70B becomes the *highest* of the three" does not survive**: like-for-like the
  ordering is 3B +0.0745 > 70B +0.0681 > 8B +0.0090. Still non-monotone (the 8B is the outlier), so
  F-102's actual conclusion — that the clean monotone ordering is a mean-pooling artifact — stands.
- **The sign reversal against raw survives but shrinks**: −0.0178 / −0.0742 / **+0.0069** instead of
  −0.0178 / −0.0742 / +0.0333. Persona still loses to raw at both small rungs under `last` and is
  barely positive at 70B.
