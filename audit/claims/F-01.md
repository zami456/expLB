# F-01 — "HP-KR is largely a lexical task"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:21` (status in doc: *verified*). Batch P1-hpkr.
**Importance:** HIGH — it is the reason the whole `kd/` experiment and the matched slice exist.

## The claim, stated precisely
A single binary feature — "does the assistant's last message contain a knowledge denial",
defined by the regex `\b(do not|don't|doesn't|not)\b[^.]{0,20}\bknow\b` minus a leading
`yes`/`I know` — scores **AUROC 0.913** against the `deceptive` column of
`results/subsets/harm-pressure-knowledge-report.csv`, restricted to the
`llama-v3.3-70b-instruct` rows (2,139 of 7,068). It supersedes an earlier **0.870** computed
over all 7,068 rows. Caveat quoted: "68 deceptive rows it does not catch".

No layer, bank, pooling, seed or probe is involved — this is a text feature against a label.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/F-01_F-02_F-16.py` reads the CSV directly. The regex, the
`yes`/`I know` guard, the model filter and the row-id construction are **transcribed** from
`src/assistant_axis_ndif/eval_datasets.py` (lines 363–364, 385–420, 126–160) rather than
imported, so the check is independent of the project's code path. AUROC is rank-based
(Mann–Whitney U) with ties at 0.5.

## What does not check out
The caveat **"68 deceptive rows it does not catch"** belongs to the **superseded all-rows**
computation, not to the 0.913 it is attached to. On the Llama rows the regex misses **15**
deceptive rows, not 68. The entry mixes the two row sets in one bullet list — the same error
F-16 later flags for F-01's and F-02's *counts*, but it was never applied to this caveat.

## Robustness (not asked for by the claim, recorded anyway)
The feature is binary, so AUROC = balanced accuracy. On the Llama rows TPR 0.892 / FPR 0.067;
on all rows TPR 0.952 / FPR 0.212. The 0.913-vs-0.870 gap is driven by the far lower false
positive rate on the Llama rows, i.e. the other two generators deny knowledge much more often
while being labelled honest.

## Verdict
**HOLDS** — confidence **HIGH** (recomputed from the released CSV with independent code; both
the current and the superseded figure reproduce to three decimals).
One wrong detail: the "68" caveat is the all-rows figure; the Llama-row figure is 15.
