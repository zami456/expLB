**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:3992` (2026-09-21) plus its 23:05 amendment.
Batch P7-register-sarcasm.

## The claim, in one sentence
With a bag-of-words logistic regression grouped by question id (no item spans train and test)
over 300 single-turn rollouts (3 arms × 5 variants × 20 questions, Llama-3.3-70B, temp 0.7,
one seed), ironic vs sycophantic responses separate at **0.953 raw / 0.947 markers-stripped**
over all variants (n=200), **1.000 / 1.000** on v1 alone (n=40), and — decisively — still
**0.919 / 0.921** on v4+v5 where the ironic marker rate is 0.00 (n=80), with ironic vs sincere
at 0.997 / 0.996; the discriminating words are ordinary content vocabulary (abstract and
first-person on the ironic side, concrete and second-person on the sycophantic side), which
makes the would-be bank a register axis; and the amendment's polarity check passes with all
three arms at 100% / 96% / 100% scoring ≥2.

## The missing confidence interval, now measured
F-93's caveat (1) says the 0.921 "has no confidence interval — it should be bootstrapped over
questions". I did that: resampling the 20 question ids with replacement (B = 2,000, seed 42) on
the fixed out-of-fold scores gives

```
    point (out-of-fold) 0.9213
    95% CI over questions [0.8719, 0.9669]   P(<= 0.65 pre-registered ceiling) = 0.0000
```

The interval sits far above the design's 0.65 ceiling in every resample. **The caveat is
discharged and it supports the verdict**: the gate fails with room to spare.

## Verdict: HOLDS
All four separability rows, all three arms' marker rates, all marker counts and the entire
polarity table reproduce exactly from the original responses and judge scores, under two
independent implementations, and the one caveat that could have softened the verdict now has a
number that hardens it.

One provenance nuance, not an error in the result: **F-93's ironic feature list is the
*unstripped* fit.** It quotes `…, definitely, …`, but `definitely` is in the scorer's
`MARKER_TOKENS` and cannot appear in a markers-stripped model. Re-fitting both ways:
```
  strip_markers=False  ironic top-12: of, that's, ability, undoubtedly, i, i'm, definitely, fact, decision, like, a, in
  strip_markers=True   ironic top-12: of, that's, undoubtedly, ability, i, i'm, fact, decision, like, a, that, in
```
F-93's list matches the first; F-105's corrected list matches the second. The register reading
is identical either way.
