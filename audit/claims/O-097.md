# O-097 — "Two gates were registered in the design document, with thresholds, before generation" (G2: bag-of-words AUROC ≤ 0.65)

**Claim (restated).** `docs/thesis/sarcasm-pilot-negative-result.md:70-75`: two abandon-conditions
were **registered in the design document, with thresholds, before generation** — G1 lowest ironic
marker rate ≤ 0.20, **G2 bag-of-words AUROC ≤ 0.65, grouped by item** — and G2's rationale is given
as *"the design's own words: 'near 0.5 with markers removed = the arms are textually alike, which is
the premise the factorial needs. Near 1.0 = a lexical detector.'"*
This is the pre-registration claim on which the section's whole framing ("a pre-registered design
that failed its own gate") rests.

**What I did.** `audit/scripts/claims/W6D_misc.py`: searched every committed version of the design
document and the two pilot scripts for the thresholds, and dated them against the generation window
recorded in the chain's own status files.

**Findings.**
1. **0.65 is in no version of `cap/docs/SARCASM_DESIGN.md`, including the current one.** It exists
   only in `run_sarc_pilot.ps1:90,102,108,112` and `score_sarcasm_markers.py:331 (SEP_MAX = 0.65)`.
2. **0.65 post-dates the data and post-dates the first printing of the separability number.**
   Generation finished 21:40:53; the chain printed `stripped=0.947` at 21:44:27; 0.65 first enters
   the repository at 21:48:54.
3. **G1's 0.20 was in the chain before the number existed** (`aea9f53`, 21:25:55) but is also **not
   in the design document**.
4. **The "design's own words" quoted for G2 are not the design document's.** That sentence is the
   print statement at `cap/scripts/score_sarcasm_markers.py:198-199`.
5. **What the design document actually registered.** v1 (`3d80071`, before any generation) did
   register a separability abandon-condition — *"If it separates sarcastic from sincere above ~0.75,
   the wording is not matched and the bank is dead regardless of what the activations say"* — but
   v2 (`5ced550`, 19:03, still before generation) **removed the number**: *"report **selectivity** —
   judged-sarcasm signal minus bag-of-words separability — rather than a bare number against an
   arbitrary 0.75."* So at generation time the design document deliberately had **no numeric
   separability threshold**.

**The conclusion survives; the framing does not.** Under the only threshold that *was*
pre-registered (v1's ~0.75) the measured values — 0.947 all-variant and 0.921 on the unmarked
v4+v5 subset (recomputed in O-098/O-100) — still fail, and by a wide margin. So the gate failure is
real. What cannot be said is that 0.65 was "registered in the design document, with thresholds,
before generation". The honest restatement: *the design registered an abandon-condition on lexical
separability before generation (v1, ~0.75), v2 replaced it with a selectivity criterion, and the
0.65 operationalisation was written into the chain after the first number was printed; the measured
0.921–0.947 fails every version of the bar.*

**Related, already on record.** §5 of the same document concedes that the *gate rule* was absent on
09-21 and that the fix reached the lab machine only on 09-22 (F-104). This check shows the
threshold itself, not only its enforcement, was chosen after the number.

**Verdict: DOES NOT HOLD** as written (the G2 threshold was not registered in the design document

Confidence HIGH — commit timestamps and byte counts, not prose.
**Importance HIGH** — this is the methodological claim of the project's flagship negative result,
