## Verdict — HOLDS
- **The before/after evidence is exact.** The preserved 09-21 status file logs
  `VERDICT PRODUCIBLE` one line above `stripped=0.947`, and the 09-22 02:29 file contains the
  three-line 3a/3b/VERDICT block the entry quotes almost verbatim (the entry drops ", markers" from
  the 3b line).
- **The byte sizes are independently confirmed by git.** `run_sarc_pilot.ps1` at `aea9f53` is
  **5,688 B** — the lab copy — and at `b34ff2e` is **6,755 B** — the repo copy. `run_cap_controls.ps1`
  goes 7,993 → 8,362 B at `080a979` = **+369 B**.
- **"All four deployed and hash-matched" holds, and is corroborated from an unexpected direction.**
  At the audit's census (18 h later) all **153** scripts present in both places were md5-identical
  after line-ending normalisation, including all four named files, and **0** remote-only files
  remained. The audit later found the RF-14 guard bug live on *both* machines and kept pre-patch
  copies of both: `originals_lab` and `originals_repo` are identical for all 8 guard scripts —
  which is itself proof that the two trees were in sync, not evidence against F-104.

Two pieces that cannot be, or no longer can be, verified:
1. **The 160/151 counts are a 2026-09-21 20:30 snapshot** and the earliest census the audit has is
   09-22 20:56, which gives **170 local / 153 remote** (10 and 2 scripts were added in between).
   The *structure* matches — 17 local-only then, 17 (+3 in `tests/`) now.
2. **The `cap_controls.status` lines the entry quotes are gone.** That file was truncated and
   rewritten at 09-22 16:05/16:10 by the boot-trigger wave through the RF-14 guard bug, so neither
   the NaN gate failure nor the "$src/$SRC" re-run line survives. The entry's conclusion about that
   bug is still supported by the `$vsrc` diff visible in git, but the log evidence is `NO RAW DATA`.

**Stale caveat.** Caveat (2), "the 8 remote-only registration scripts are still uncommitted", was
already being falsified as it was written: six `register_*.ps1` entered git in **`4d0d046`
(2026-09-22)** — the same commit that carries F-104 — and by the census no lab-only script remained
in those four directories. → `DOC_FIXES.md`.
