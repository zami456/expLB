# O-093 — "Persona methods: layer 40 dominates. Median +0.113 (13 methods), up to +0.234 zero-shot."

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:744–746`, verdict bullet 3 of §7.6
(inventory cites `:738`; +6-line offset, see O-076). Batch **W6B-thesis2**.

## The claim, restated
For the **13** persona-based method rows of `q6_layers.json`, the gain from moving to layer 40 from
the better of layers 20/22 has median **+0.113**, with a maximum of **+0.234** on a zero-shot method;
and the accompanying sentence says this handicaps persona methods "about 4×" more than raw probes.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/W6B_q6.py` — same rebuilt raw/persona split as O-092, medians and maxima
recomputed from the 23 stored `gap_40_minus_best_of_20_22` values; the 4× ratio recomputed as
persona-median / raw-median. `audit/scripts/claims/W6B_robust.py` splits the persona group by
protocol.

## Robustness (verbatim, `audit/phase2/out/W6B/W6B_robust.txt`)

```
  persona : n=13 median +0.1135
  persona, trained rows only              : n=8 median +0.1135
  persona, zero-shot rows only            : n=5 median +0.0665
```

The median is unchanged if the five zero-shot rows are dropped (+0.1135 either way), so — unlike
O-092's raw median — it is **not** an artefact of pooling protocols. But the zero-shot subgroup on
its own is much weaker (+0.067), and one zero-shot row is **negative** (per-token top-10 %, −0.028).
So "layer 40 dominates" is a statement about the eight trained persona rows.

## Verdict — **HOLDS**
Median, maximum, count and the 4× ratio all reproduce exactly.

**Confidence:** HIGH — recomputed from the 23 per-row gaps with an independently rebuilt split.
**Importance:** HIGH — this is the mechanism the thesis uses to explain why persona coordinates look
better at layer 40 than at the paper's layer; it directly supports the layer choice behind the
headline persona-vs-raw comparisons.

**For the reframed thesis:** pair the +0.113 with the two dataset-level tests that measure the same
thing and come back non-significant (q 0.097 and 0.456). A large median over 13 hand-grouped method
rows and a non-significant dataset bootstrap are the same finding seen two ways.
