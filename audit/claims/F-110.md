## The claim in one sentence
`run-crossgen/scale_70b/` mixes two persona banks under the single label `bank_name=paper16_70b`
— the L40 matrices are the **paper16** bank, the other 40 are the **36-persona kd** bank — and when
the 70B sweep is re-run like-for-like on the paper16 bank, the three-rung layer-mean margin over the
matched dataset-PCA control is **+0.1197 / +0.1027 / +0.0541** (3B / 8B / 70B) and the 70B margin over
raw activations turns **negative, −0.0131**, instead of F-100's +0.0751 and +0.0079.
Metric: mean off-diagonal AUROC over 20 train/test pairs, the paper's five datasets
(instructed-deception, mask, convincing-game, ai_liar, roleplaying), k=3, `probe=paper`, seed 42,
**mean** pooling, F-100's 19-layer set (all stored layers except L72 and L76),
`best = per-layer max(axes, pc:deception)`.

## Two things to record honestly
1. **The `last`-pooling figures quoted in F-110 (+0.0069 / +0.0681) are the 21-layer means, while its
   main table is F-100's 19-layer set.** The entry does not say which. The 19-layer `last` values are
   **+0.0061 / +0.0688** — the difference changes nothing, but the mixed layer set should be stated.
2. **The corrected decline is still a mean-pooling result.** Like-for-like under `last` pooling the three
   rungs read **+0.0745 / +0.0090 / +0.0688** on the matched control — non-monotone, with the 8B the
   outlier. F-110 mentions the pooling contrast only in passing ("holds with smaller margins"); the
   headline "the decline with model size is +0.120 → +0.103 → +0.054, steeper than reported" is true
   under mean pooling only, exactly as F-102 warned about F-63 and F-100.
