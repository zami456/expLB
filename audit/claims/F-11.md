# F-11 — "The kd contrast carries a large Assistant-Axis drift component"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
On the **s3** kd bank at **layer 40**, the mean projection of each group's centroid onto the
**assistant axis** is `knowledge_denial` **−0.22**, `sincere_ignorance` **+1.60**,
`plain_assertion` **+0.90**, and the paper16 groups inside the same bank
`deception` **1.51** / `honesty` **2.20**. The two sides of the primary knowledge-denial
contrast are therefore **1.82 apart**, against **0.69** for paper16's own deception contrast.
Also `cos(PC1, assistant)` = **0.583** and the `default` anchor sits at relative position
**0.882** on PC1 rather than at an extreme.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:179-196`, citing `logs/kd_axes.log`, printed by `6_axes.py:265`.
Print-only: no result file holds these numbers.

## Robustness — the number is strongly layer-dependent, the conclusion is not
The entry gives one layer. `audit/scripts/claims/P6_lab_d.py` sweeps all 21 stored layers:

| layer | 0 | 16 | 20 | 32 | **40** | 44 | 56 | 76 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| kd gap | 0.03 | 0.52 | 0.86 | 1.32 | **1.82** | 2.14 | 3.10 | 4.50 |
| paper16 deception gap | 0.01 | 0.13 | 0.26 | 0.51 | **0.69** | 0.78 | 1.10 | 1.62 |
| ratio | 3.0 | 4.0 | 3.3 | 2.6 | **2.64** | 2.74 | 2.82 | 2.78 |

The absolute gap grows monotonically with depth (activation norms grow), so "1.82" is an
L40 quantity with no meaning elsewhere. The **qualitative** claim — the kd contrast's
assistant-axis separation is ~2.6–4× the paper16 deception contrast's — holds at **every**
stored layer. `cos(PC1, assistant)` stays in 0.54–0.70 across layers, never reaching the
Assistant-Axis paper's 0.71.

The `s23` control (F-19) is the claim's own stated check and it is reported separately.
The third arm, the **unfiltered** `axes_vectors_all.pt` bank that shares the log, gives a gap
of **1.60** at L40 — below both s3 (1.82) and s23 (1.67), i.e. 88% of the s3 gap survives
removing the score filter entirely. The ledger does not mention this arm.
