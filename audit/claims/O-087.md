Two findings the table row does not carry:

1. **Two different pooled numbers exist in the project for the same quantity.** `0.863` comes from
   `12_dotproduct_layers.py`, which skips single-class datasets (`if len(np.unique(y)) < 2:
   continue`, i.e. alpaca is dropped). `0.881` comes from `25_zeroshot_nulls.py`, which keeps
   alpaca's 2,000 honest-only rows in the pooled set (only its per-dataset loop requires two
   classes). Both exclude `sycophancy_mt`. Neither is wrong; they are different corpora, and nothing
   in either document says which.
2. **Per dataset the axis beats a 1,000-random-direction null on only 5 of 9 datasets.** It is at or
   below chance on `mask` (0.467) and `harm-pressure-choice` (0.544). The pooled 0.863 is therefore
   substantially a *between-dataset* effect — which is exactly what §7.12's "dataset identity is
   99.3 % decodable" predicts.
