## Robustness / caveats
- **This row is a pooled-corpus number, not a transfer mean.** `dotproducts_mean.json`'s `matched`
  values are the AUROC of one axis over *all Pass-B rows concatenated*, whereas the trained rows in
  the same table (including O-086's) are means over 90–110 off-diagonal source→target cells.
  Within a row — which is what §7.6 asks — that is fine; across rows the table is not like-for-like,
  and the thesis does not flag it per row.
- Reconciling with §8.2: that section quotes the pooled-corpus zero-shot deception axis at L40 mean
  as **0.881** "incl. alpaca" (from `zeroshot_nulls.json`), against 0.877 here, because
  `12_dotproduct_layers.py` **excludes** single-class sets. The two are different row sets, ~0.004
  apart; neither section says so.
- §8.2 also records that the zero-shot deception-axis readings sit **inside** the random-direction
  null in several tests, so the L40 0.877 should not be read as "significantly better than a random
  direction" without that section's per-test verdict.
- One seed, mean pooling only; a `last` sweep of the same quantity is not in `run-coords`.
