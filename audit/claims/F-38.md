# F-38 — "gender-secret and soft-trigger are fine-tune datasets — structurally excluded"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:985` (2026-09-20). Batch **P9-findings-rest**.

## The claim, in one sentence
Checked in the `model` column: `gender-secret` was generated by
`llama-v3.3-70b-instruct-secret-male` (fine-tuned), `soft-trigger` by
`llama-3.3-70b-it-lora-gender` (LoRA), `instructed-deception` by `llama-v3.3-70b-instruct`
(base) — so the first two cannot be replicated at 3B/8B without training adapters, and,
uniquely, their rows were written by a fine-tuned model while their activations are read from
the **base** 70B on NDIF; the 11-dataset grid therefore stays at 11.
