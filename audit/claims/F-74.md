0.1898, the measured −0.7684, the analytic null −0.7462, the slope −0.0998 → −0.100, r −0.0671

The two Monte-Carlo quantities reproduce within their own sampling error: my permuted null
mean −0.7455 (F-74: −0.7456) with 95% [−0.8001, −0.6889] (F-74: [−0.7986, −0.6892]), and

different RNG streams; a difference of 0.002 in a tail probability estimated from 20,000
draws is ordinary noise (binomial s.e. ≈ 0.003). The conclusion — the measured value lies
inside the null — is unaffected.
