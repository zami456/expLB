# F-20 — "The matched slice is reportable on passB ONLY"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
`harm-pressure-kr-matched` (the knowledge-denial-matched slice of HP-KR) has very different
composition depending on the activation store:

| store | HP-KR rows | matched rows | deceptive / honest |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **passB** | 2,139 | **258** | **124 / 134** — balanced |
| passA | 200 | **98** | **89 / 9** |
| passC | 200 | **98** | **89 / 9** |

Because passA and passC are 2,000-row subsamples whose HP-KR rows were label-stratified to
100/100, and HP-KR's honest class is overwhelmingly *"I know the answer"* assertions — which by
construction cannot appear in a denial-matched slice — only 9 honest rows survive. So the
passA/passC grids run with **10 datasets, not 11**, and a matched-slice number must be quoted
from passB only.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:342-366`, citing `make_view_ac.py` over
`run-coords/passA|passC/harm-pressure-knowledge-report/rows.jsonl`. The 98 and 89:9 were
**stdout only**.

## 2. The passA/passC 98-row, 89:9 state: NO RAW DATA — but corroborated two ways
That state no longer exists. Since 2026-09-21 the passA and passC HP-KR stores were topped up
to the full 2,139 rows (mtimes 11:29:40 and 11:52:44), and the kr-matched views were rebuilt on
**2026-09-22 15:47:01** (the RF-13 boot-trigger wave). All three stores now read **2,139 → 258
{124 / 134}**, and `logs/passAC11_view.log` records the rebuild in those words. `make_view_ac.py`
never wrote its counts to a file, so the literal "98" and "89 / 9" survive nowhere.

Two independent reconstructions put the claim's numbers where it says they were:

**(a) The surviving pre-top-up split ids.** `run-crossgen/passA/splits.json` (117,438 B,
**2026-09-19 14:16**, i.e. before the top-up) stores the seed-42 HP-KR test set: **40 row_ids**
(20% of a 200-row store — consistent with the 200 the claim reports). Of those 40, **20 are in
the matched slice, with labels {deceptive 18, honest 2}**. Scaled to the full 200-row store
that is ~100 matched rows in a ~90:10 ratio. `run-crossgen/passC/splits.json` (2026-09-18) and
both `kd11/pass{A,C}/splits.json` give the same 20 / 18:2. The five other seeds in the passA
and passC files give 16–20 matched of 40, at 15:1 to 17:3.

**(b) The hypergeometric expectation.** Drawing 100 deceptive rows from the 139 in the corpus,
of which 124 are in the matched slice, expects **89.2** (sd ≈ 1.6); drawing 100 honest from
2,000, of which 134 are matched, expects **6.7** (sd ≈ 2.4). F-20's **89 / 9** sits on both
expectations.

So the numbers are not recomputable, but they are exactly what the surviving evidence predicts.
Verdict for this sub-claim: **NO RAW DATA, corroborated**.

200-row, label-stratified 100/100 premise the claim rests on.
