## Robustness

- This is the `lr_reg10` companion to O-071's `mms` row. Under both probes on Pass A the persona
  axes fail to beat raw anywhere at or above layer 20 except `mms` at L44 (+0.0140).
- Broad sweep over `audit/phase2/layer_sensitivity.csv` (crossgen family, layers ≥ 20, 5,848
  cells with both features present): `axes` beats `raw` in 1,419 cells (24.3 %), median
  `axes − raw` = **−0.0447**. By layer: L20 median +0.0003 (127/252 positive), L40 −0.0101
  (94/228), L44 −0.0125 (83/240), L60 −0.0683 (29/152). Same-layer axes-vs-raw is therefore
  negative on the median in every band except 20, across banks, poolings, seeds and probes.
- Single seed (42) for this file; no seed spread exists for the Pass A `run-coords` sweep.
