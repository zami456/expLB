## The claim, in one sentence
A standing misreading was corrected: 'Next=none' on a Windows scheduled task does not mean the task cannot fire, only that no time-based run is scheduled; a boot trigger does not populate NextRunTime. Roughly twenty status readings had asserted the wrong thing.

## What the claim says
That `Next=none` was misread as "this task cannot fire", across roughly twenty status readings.
