# F-08 — "Scoring WMDP ourselves gives a cleaner knowledge label"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:126` (status in doc: *verified*). Batch P1-hpkr.
**Importance:** MEDIUM-HIGH — it is the evidence for F-02's caveat that HP-KR's
`model_belief == -1` is not an ignorance measure, and it is the input F-09 rests on.

## The claim, stated precisely
1,459 WMDP items scored by the model's own A/B/C/D logits under a neutral prompt:
accuracy **0.786** (bio **0.867**, cyber **0.736**); predictions spread **350/425/375/309**;
HP-KR's `model_belief == -1` marks **119** items, the model gets **312** wrong, and the two
measures **disagree on 24%** of items — "**2.6×**, not the 3–5× I predicted".
Source: `kd/run/knowledge/scores.jsonl`.

## The version question comes first
`kd/run/knowledge/scores.jsonl` is one of RF-5's truncated files: the **committed copy is
exactly 204,800 bytes** (the known scp 200 KiB cut) and holds 1,095 parseable records plus
one cut mid-record. The **lab copy is 279,757 bytes / 1,459 records**, and it is already in
`audit/cache/labpc/kd/run/knowledge/scores.jsonl` (MANIFEST md5
`4b7e503b9356c7440db65152e1ab5f94`, MATCH). Lab mtime 2026-09-19 17:22 — before the
2026-09-22 wave. **I used the lab copy**, and I ran the truncated one too, to show what a
naive recheck would have concluded.

## The trap this claim sits in
Against the **committed** copy nothing matches: 0.817 / 0.873 / 0.767 / 288-300-271-236 /
90 / 200 / 20.8%. Anyone re-checking F-08 from the repo alone would report it as failing.
The finding is right and the *published artefact* is broken — a DOC_FIXES item, not a claim
problem. The same file backs F-09 and (per Phase 1) F-59.

## A detail worth keeping
The two "does not know" measures overlap far less than the headline suggests: of the 119
items HP-KR marks `-1` and the 312 the model gets wrong, only **42** are in both sets. The
disagreement is not just "one is bigger"; they largely pick out different items.
