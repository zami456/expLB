## Level 1

Doc "0.005–0.018" vs file min 0.0053 / max 0.0177 → rounds to exactly 0.005 and 0.018.
**Match**, tolerance 5e-4. The claim that the signal *survives* is supported: every erased
transfer AUROC stays between 0.646 and 0.712, i.e. all six costs are under 2 % of the base.

## Level 2

Not recomputed from activations — re-fitting the erased probes needs the ~30 GB Pass B
activation store, which is lab/Drive-only. Internal consistency was checked instead: the six
`cost_assistant` values equal `raw − erase_assistant` to the printed precision, and the
`assistant erasure vs random erasures` comparisons give the same diffs (+0.0061, +0.0050,
+0.0177, +0.0139, +0.0081, +0.0049), so the random-erasure baseline really is ≈0.

## Robustness / caveats for the thesis

- **The "only" is doing work in two directions.** Three of the six settings have a
  BH-significant cost (L40 mean mms q 0.0035; L44 last mms q 0.0035; L44 last lr_reg10 q 0.0399)
  — so "only 0.005–0.018" is small in size but not statistically negligible. §7.4's own verdict
  says this; the one-line §1.2 summary does not.
- **The erased direction is paper16's own assistant direction**, cos 0.30–0.37 with the
  published Assistant Axis (§10, and confirmed in O-057 above: cos(paper16 dec, published) is a
  different object entirely). The doc flags this. The claim "signal survives removal of *the*
  Assistant Axis" is therefore **not** tested.
- §7.4's verdict says the assistant direction carries "1–3 % of transfer AUROC"; the measured
  range is **0.80–2.64 %**. Two of six settings are below 1 %.
