**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:3290` (batch P2-pooled). **Importance: HIGH** (it closes F-81's
last caveat and is the entry the draft would have to answer on robustness).

## The claim, restated
1. **Seed stability.** The pooled-LODO − single-source margin at L40 (mean pooling, `paper` probe,
   11 datasets) across five split seeds: 1 → +0.1728, 7 → +0.1979, 13 → +0.1845, 42 → +0.1782,
   99 → +0.1777; range +0.173 to +0.198, **spread 0.025**, "smaller than the margin's own
   bootstrap CI width (+0.143 to +0.213)".
2. **Mahalanobis.** At L40 over 110 pairs: plain cosine **R² = 0.244**; Mahalanobis with the
   **within-class** covariance **R² = 0.023**; Mahalanobis with the **total** covariance
   **R² = 0.017** — against Ying et al.'s 0.56 / 0.98.

Read all five root seed files and both `mahal/` files from the md5-verified cache; recomputed the
bootstrap CI width myself (it is stored nowhere). Script: `audit/scripts/claims/p2p_premise_core.py`.
