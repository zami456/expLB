# H-010 -- the persona axis's advantage is carried almost entirely by HP-KR

**Source:** `src/probes/deception-detection/results/loo_L20/summary.md:53-54` (commit `6b16a46`,
2026-09-20).

## The claim in one sentence

Almost all of the extended persona axis's mean advantage over pooled supervision comes from
harm-pressure-knowledge-report, where the pooled baseline "inverts" to 0.124 at layer 20 and 0.523
at layer 40.

## Part 1 -- the advantage really is carried by HP-KR (holds)

```
-- layer 20 --  ext_axis1 mean 0.9041 excl-HPKR 0.9086   (advantage +0.1098 -> -0.0197 excl-HPKR)
-- layer 40 --  ext_axis1 mean 0.9158 excl-HPKR 0.9165   (advantage +0.0662 -> +0.0016 excl-HPKR)
```

(advantage = persona axis minus the better pooling at that depth, over all six held-out sets, then
over the five excluding HP-KR; recomputed Level 2 from `loo_scores.npz`.)

Dropping one dataset out of six turns +0.110 into -0.020 at L20 and +0.066 into +0.002 at L40. The
claim is not just supported, it is understated: at layer 20 the advantage does not shrink, it
**reverses**.

## Part 2 -- the numbers are right, but "inverts" is wrong at layer 40

```
   HP-KR pooled baseline, L20: naive 0.3254, balanced 0.1243  (below 0.5? naive=True,  balanced=True)
   HP-KR pooled baseline, L40: naive 0.5230, balanced 0.5612  (below 0.5? naive=False, balanced=False)
```

- **0.124** = `loo_L20/single_source_reference.csv` / `loo_bootstrap.csv`, HP-KR,
  `pooled_loo_balanced` = **0.1243**. Below chance: inverted. Correct.
- **0.523** = `loo_L40/...`, HP-KR, `pooled_loo_**naive**` = **0.5230**. That is **above** 0.5.
  It is a near-chance score, not an inversion. The balanced pooling at L40 gives 0.5612, also above
  chance. **At layer 40 the pooled baseline does not invert on HP-KR at all.**

Both values are Level-2 reproduced from the raw held-out scores.

Two further things the sentence hides:
- the two quoted values come from **different poolings** (balanced at L20, naive at L40);
- the L40 *worst* dataset for the balanced pool is not HP-KR but HP-C (0.4887) -- see H-013.

## Robustness / cross-check

His own `loo_bootstrap.csv` (balanced, 1000 stratified draws) confirms the direction and size:
HP-KR delta = -0.7573 [-0.8161, -0.6911] at L20 and -0.3512 [-0.4001, -0.2976] at L40, the two
largest magnitudes at each depth. The bootstrap also shows the pooled baseline **beating** the
persona axis on CG at both depths (+0.0302, +0.0854) and on HP-C at L20 (+0.1160), which he states.

## Verdict

**PARTLY MATCHES HIS DATA.** The substantive half (the advantage is carried by HP-KR) is verified
and if anything understated. The parenthetical is wrong: **0.523 is above chance**, so
"the pooled baseline inverts to ... 0.523 (L40)" is contradicted by his own file. The two numbers
also silently switch pooling between depths.

Confidence HIGH. Importance HIGH -- this is the strongest self-criticism in his note and the
sentence the paper is most likely to quote; the word "inverts" must not travel to layer 40.
