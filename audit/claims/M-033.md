## Notes for the lead

One precision point. `random` here is a **trained k=5 random orthonormal subspace probe**, not "a random direction"; `axes` is a trained 4-d projection. The comparison is therefore like-for-like in training and near-matched in rank (5 vs 4), which is what makes it damaging — but it is NOT the zero-shot random-direction null used elsewhere in this project (`run-coords/zeroshot_nulls.json`, `audit/phase2/controls.csv`). A future session must not quote 0.698 as a zero-shot null.
