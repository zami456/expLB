## Level 1 / Level 2

**(b) holds exactly** in the run-coords family the document is drawing on: `pc:deception` never
beats `raw` at any layer ≥ 20 in any of the four sweeps (66 layer × sweep cells). The doc's
companion sentence at line 589-590 — "The only exceptions are near-chance layers 0–16, by at
most 0.023" — also holds: the four exceptions are at layers 0, 8, 16, 0, and the largest is
+0.0223.

**(a) is DOC-ONLY and approximate.** The "+0.10" is an audit-authored reading off Figure 10
panel A of the paper, recorded in `docs/papers/persona-coordinates-notes.md` as "≈ +0.10". The
thesis prints it as "+0.10" with the "≈" dropped. I did not open the paper PDF during this batch,
so I can confirm only that the thesis number matches the audit's own note, not that the note
matches the figure.

## The comparison is not like-for-like — this is the important finding

1. **Different k.** The paper's deception PC projection is **k = 1** (the thesis says so itself
   at §2.7: "The paper used k = 1 for deception"). Our `pc:deception` is **k = 5** in every
   run-coords sweep and in 12,128 of the 12,812 crossgen cells. A 5-dimensional projection is a
   different estimator from a 1-dimensional one, and the table compares them side by side without
   saying so.
2. **Different layer regime.** The paper's number is at layer 14 of a 3B model (≈ the midpoint);
   our "layers ≥ 20" band starts at a quarter depth of an 80-layer model, and our sweeps *do*
   show pc-over-raw gains below layer 20 (up to +0.0223), which is nearer the paper's relative
   depth than the band we quote.
3. **Different dataset set and pipeline.** 20 off-diagonal pairs (5 datasets) there, 72 here.
   Notably, the only places in the whole crossgen census where `pc:deception` beats `raw` by more
   than +0.06 are the **5-dataset, paper-question, k = 3** cells of the 70B scale rung — i.e. the
   closest thing we have to the paper's own setting. That is a substantive qualification of
   "never positive", not a contradiction of it as stated.
4. RF-16 applies to the `scale_70b` rows just quoted: that store mixes two persona banks under
   one label. The k = 3 rows above are at layers 20/22/28, which RF-16 identifies as the kd-bank
   part of the sweep. They are context, not evidence for the paper's setting.

## Verdict

**PARTLY HOLDS.** Our half ("never positive at layers ≥ 20") is verified exhaustively — 66 of 66
cells in the run-coords family, and 94.4 % of 5,848 cells in the wider census. The paper half
("+0.10") is DOC-ONLY, an approximate figure-read, printed without its "≈". And the two columns
are not measuring the same estimator: the paper's is k = 1, ours is k = 5.

*Confidence:* HIGH for our column; LOW-MEDIUM for the paper column (figure-read, not re-checked
against the PDF this session).
*Importance:* HIGH — this row is the paper-vs-us verdict on the replicated method's headline
feature, and in a reframed thesis it becomes a central negative result. It must not be printed
until the k = 1 / k = 5 mismatch is either fixed (run k = 1) or stated.
