## Two things the note has to say plainly
1. **The "+0.184" is not our number.** It is `0.916 − 0.732`, both from the collaborator's
   abstract. Our own single-source off-diagonal on the six at L40 is **0.6960**, against which the
   same contrast would lead by **+0.220**. F-85's caveat (1) already says the honest comparison is
   ours-against-ours; the table mixing the two is what F-97 then corrects.
2. **New, and it cuts against the entry's own "layer 20 is where the claim is strongest".** The
   dataset-level bootstrap that F-85 never ran shows that **at L20 the pooled-vs-single margin on
   the six does not clear** (+0.093, CI crosses zero, P = 0.089), while at L40 it clears
   comfortably. So the layer where the persona contrast looks best is also the layer where our own
   pooled baseline is least securely measured — n = 6 is a very small resampling unit, exactly as
   F-85's caveat (3) predicted.
