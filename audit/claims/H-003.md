## Quantifier check

"a third probe family to do so" depends on how families are counted. At layer 20 on HP-KR, these
arms in his own files are below 0.5:

```
      which OTHER arms invert (AUROC<0.5) on HP-KR at L20:
         auroc_axis           0.1483
         auroc_pc1            0.2530
         auroc_syc_axis       0.0429
         auroc_syc_pc1        0.0406
         base probe (Mean Probe)   0.109
```

Six arms invert. "Third family" is defensible only if all four persona arms (deception axis,
persona PC1, sycophancy axis, sycophancy PC1) are counted as one family. Under that grouping the
three families are: trained mean probe / published persona directions / follow-up probe. The
extended (12v13) and knowledge-denial (4v6) axes do **not** invert (0.8816 and 0.8899) -- which is
the point of the extended bank.
