## Verdict — HOLDS
The measured core — 800 rows in exactly 400 chunks, 2.0 rows/chunk, and 8.0 rows/chunk on the 8B
— reproduces **exactly**, and that is what the correction turns on. The derived rates differ by
2–4% depending on where the interval is bracketed (I start the 3B clock at the "800 to do" line and
the 8B clock at its equivalent), giving 2.32x where the entry says 2.4x — the same conclusion.
The forward projection landed within four minutes of the actual finish.

One small slip: "1,554 rows remain" understates the real remainder. The 3B arm's other four
datasets are 800 + 267 + 270 + 371 = **1,708** rows, which took 20:09:05 → 21:24:14 = 75 min
(22.8 rows/min). The finish-time prediction was still right because the rate estimate was slightly
optimistic in the opposite direction.
