## The claim in one sentence

Averaged over the six held-out datasets, a probe trained on a single other dataset reaches mean
AUROC 0.648 at layer 20 and 0.732 at layer 40, against 0.694 / 0.850 for a probe pooled naively
over the other five and 0.794 / 0.804 for the same pool with per-dataset weights -- so pooled
supervision really is the stronger baseline.
