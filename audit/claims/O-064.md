# O-064 — the bf16 count bug moved persona-bank cosines by ≤ 0.001

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:357` (§3.3, "Engineering facts that
changed results").

**Claim as stated:** "The count bug moved persona-bank cosines by ≤ 0.001, because a wrong
denominator rescales without rotating. The argmax bug returned another token's activation
entirely."

**Restated:** a response mask summed in bfloat16 miscounts spans over 256 tokens, so mean-pooled
persona vectors were divided by a slightly wrong denominator. The claim is that this changed the
cosines between persona-bank axes by at most 0.001, because a per-vector rescaling does not
rotate a direction.

## What I did

The bug was fixed by a full re-extraction in fp32 (`run-paper16/vectors_s3_fp32/`, RF-15). The
buggy vectors are still on the laptop (`run-paper16/vectors_s3/`). So I rebuilt the paper16 bank
from **both** stores with the same construction as `6_axes.py` and compared.
`audit/scripts/claims/W6A_geom.py`, output `audit/phase2/out/W6A/W6A_geom.txt`.

The sanity line matters: rebuilding the axes from the old per-persona files reproduces the
stored bank's cosine to 6 dp, so my reconstruction of `6_axes.py` is faithful.

## Level 1 / Level 2

Level 1 is not available — no result file records "the effect of the count bug"; the statement
is prose in §3.3 and in the memory note "bf16 mask reductions". Level 2 is the measurement above:
**max |Δ cos(deception, assistant)| = 0.00065 over all 80 layers**, and every axis's old-vs-new
cosine is ≥ 0.99996. So the claim's bound (≤ 0.001) holds, with the largest observed change
being 65 % of the quoted bound.

The mechanism the doc gives is also confirmed: the norms move by 0.04–0.20 % while the
directions move by ≤ 4×10⁻⁵ radians of cosine — a rescale, not a rotation.

## Caveats

1. **What I measured is old-vs-fp32, which is the count bug *plus* the dtype change** (and
   possibly other fixes in the same re-extraction). It is an upper bound on the count bug alone,
   which is the right direction for a "≤ 0.001" claim but is not an isolation of the bug.
2. The claim is about **persona-bank cosines only**. It says nothing about downstream AUROCs, and
   §3.3 does not claim it does. The argmax half of the sentence ("returned another token's
   activation entirely") concerns last-token pooling of the *evaluation* corpus and was handled
   by restarting Pass B from zero; it is not testable from the persona store and I did not test it.
3. `run-paper16/vectors_s3/` is the laptop copy; the lab replaced its copy with
   `vectors_s3_fp32/` (RF-12), so this comparison is only possible on the laptop.

## Version / RF checks

Directly on RF-12 / RF-15 territory: old = `run-paper16/vectors_s3/*.pt` (laptop = Drive, md5
identical per RF-12), new = `audit/cache/labpc/run-paper16/vectors_s3_fp32/*.pt` (copied from the
lab, mtimes 2026-09-15/16). This measurement independently confirms RF-15's own finding
("old-vs-fp32 cosine 1.0000 at every layer; max relative norm difference 0.5–0.9 %") — except
that on the **axes** I measure smaller norm changes (0.04–0.20 %) than RF-15 reports; RF-15's
0.5–0.9 % is presumably over the per-persona vectors rather than the contrast axes.

## Verdict

**HOLDS.** Measured, not just asserted: ≤ 0.00065 against a claimed ≤ 0.001.

*Confidence:* MEDIUM-HIGH — the number is a clean recompute, but it bounds the count bug
*together with* the fp32 change rather than isolating it.
*Importance:* MEDIUM — it is the sentence that licenses keeping every pre-fix persona-geometry
number (Q1, §7.1) instead of re-running it.
