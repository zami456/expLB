## Version and the BH caveat
- Both copies identical (md5 above), mtime **2026-09-17**, i.e. **before** the 2026-09-22
  15:40–16:26 regeneration wave (RF-13). This file was not in the rewrite set.
- **The q-value's family is this file's own 26 comparisons (Q3 14 + Q4 12)** — not the 4,788-test
  crossgen family whose q-values RF-13 shifted. The thesis does **not** say which family the
  "q 0.52" was corrected over; per RF-13 that is exactly the thing a reader cannot reconstruct.
  Since the file predates the wave and was not regenerated, the value is stable.
- Bank: `erase_test.json` records `axes_vectors_s3.pt` (the **old**, pre-fp32 paper16 axes, RF-15)
  for the 4-dataset pilot. RF-15's measurement — old-vs-fp32 axis cosine 1.0000 at every layer,
  ≤0.9% norm difference, sign pinning unaffected at L20/L40/L44 — bounds any effect on an
  *erasure* result at well under the 0.006 cost being reported, but the bank field of the
  `q4_table` producer itself is not recorded in the JSON, so I state it as bounded, not verified.
