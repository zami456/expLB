**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:101` (status in doc: *verified*). Batch P1-hpkr.
**Importance:** HIGH — it is the explanation that rescues F-05's failed gate cell, and it is
the origin of the project's "L20 is where the factors separate" claim, which cuts against the
"use L40" rule everything else in the repo follows.

## The claim, stated precisely
1. `cos(d_knowledge, d_utterance)` = **0.063** (L20), **0.410** (L40), **0.430** (L44).
2. Orthogonalising `d_utterance` against `d_knowledge` moves its **text-matched** AUROC
   **0.784 → 0.509** at L40 and **0.785 → 0.486** at L44 — "exactly the 0.5 predicted".
3. Therefore L20 is the layer where the two factors separate.

Mean pooling. Source: `kd/results/phase2_gate.json` "plus the orthogonalisation run".

the console output of `C:/Users/user4/expLB/ortho.py`, a loose script on the lab PC that is
in no branch and not on Drive, and that writes nothing. This is a W3 "print-only number".

## Robustness / things worth recording
- **L20 is not quoted and is interesting**: there the orthogonalisation moves 0.3512 → 0.3335,
  i.e. `d_utterance` on the text-matched slice is *inverted*, not near 0.5, both before and
  after. The entry's story ("the factors separate at L20") is about the cosine, and the
  cosine supports it (0.063 ≈ 0 against 0.41/0.43); but the claim "0.5 is what you get once
  you remove the entanglement" is only demonstrated at L40 and L44.
- Orthogonalising barely touches the *knowledge-matched* score (0.9994 → 0.9819 at L40), as
  it should: that slice is where `d_utterance` is supposed to work.
- Mean pooling only — the entry says so. Three layers only, out of 21 stored.
- `d_knowledge` and `d_utterance` are difference-of-means directions from teacher-forced
  corpora (1,962 items, 1,841/121; and 1,800 items, 900/900), not persona axes, so no
  persona-bank provenance question arises (RF-12/15/16 do not apply).
