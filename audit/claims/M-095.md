## The claim, in one plain sentence
In the same 90-cell off-diagonal grid, a **random 2-d projection** gains **+0.056** where raw
< 0.6 and loses **−0.44** where raw ≥ 0.85 (corr = **−0.76**), while **`pc:unified@2` gains +0.18**
and **`datapca@2` +0.12** in that same weak-raw bucket — so "gains where raw is weak" is partly a
ceiling / regression-to-chance effect, not evidence about the unified axis.

`UNDERSPECIFIED`: this note names neither the layer, the pooling, the probe nor the store for
these three figures (its earlier paragraph is about `run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3.pt` at layer 40).
I tested the reading the companion note `matched-control-kills-the-gain.md` states explicitly —
**L40, mean pooling, `paper` probe, `run-crossgen` root store, seed 42, 10 datasets** — and all
five numbers land.

## Robustness
Identical to M-018: probe-dependent in the weak bucket (`mms` +0.115, `lr_reg10` +0.012 for
random@2) but the sign and the strong negative correlation hold at **all 21 stored layers**
(gain +0.034…+0.137, loss −0.28…−0.52, corr −0.75…−0.88). `pc:unified@2`'s weak-bucket gain is
larger than random@2's at L40 (+0.18 vs +0.06) but its *overall* delta vs raw is still negative
(−0.034) — which is the note's point.
