**The ledger itself supersedes this entry with F-50 (`:1477`), which says 2.4x, not 1.8x.**

## The claim, restated
On the 3B evaluation arm, `instructed-deception`, 800 sampled rows: **306 chunks in 24.5 minutes**
from 19:36:40 = 12.5 chunks/min × **~2.6 rows/chunk** = **~32.5 rows/min**, against the 8B arm's
**59 rows/min** → the 3B is **1.8× slower**. Also: `items.pt` 0.34 MB (3B) "matching the 8B's 0.30 MB".

## Verdict — DOES NOT HOLD (REVISED → F-50)
Measured from the raw chunk files, the 3B arm writes **exactly 2.00 rows per chunk**, not 2.6, so its
throughput is **24.8 rows/min**, not 32.5, and the 3B:8B ratio is **2.33×**, not 1.8×. That is F-50's
correction, and F-50's numbers are the ones that reproduce. The only part of F-49 that survives is
the chunk *rate* it measured (307 chunks in the first 24.5 min vs its 306) and the qualitative point
that F-35's 9.8× did not transfer.

One extra error the ledger never corrected: **`items.pt` is byte-identical on the two rungs**
(359,384 B each), so F-49's "0.34 MB matching the 8B's 0.30 MB" is wrong in a way that reads as
confirmation. Identical span files are the *expected* result — F-54 later states that the two rungs
match row for row — so the right observation was "identical", not "0.34 vs 0.30".

**Confidence HIGH** (counts and mtimes read directly from the store). **Importance LOW** — a
throughput projection, superseded within the same day; no result depends on it. Worth keeping in the
ledger because F-50 already does the honest work of retracting it.
