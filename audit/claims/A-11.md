## "lifts transfer on the four unrelated datasets by 0.18–0.21" — reproducible, but not what it sounds like

There is exactly one reading of his files that produces 0.18–0.21, and it is the **trained**
transfer matrix at **layer 20**, comparing the extended contrast against the published 8v7 one,
column by column (mean over the five training sets):

```
L20   convince_me             axis1 0.7639  ext 0.9692   +0.2053   <-- in range
      insider_trading         axis1 0.7927  ext 0.9899   +0.1972   <-- in range
      instruct_dishonesty     axis1 0.7752  ext 0.9539   +0.1787   <-- in range
      repe_honesty            axis1 0.7961  ext 0.9908   +0.1947   <-- in range
      harm_refusal_choice     axis1 0.6301  ext 0.6393   +0.0092
      harm_refusal_kr         axis1 0.1483  ext 0.8816   +0.7333
```

So "the four unrelated datasets" = CG, IT, ID and **repe_honesty** (not HP-C, which gains +0.009),
and the range 0.179–0.205 is exact.

**But the lift is a sign artifact, not a transfer gain.** His `matrix_axis1.csv` at L20 has one
row — the probe trained on HP-KR — in which the 1-d contrast learns the *inverted* sign and
carries it everywhere:

```
                                   CG      HP-C    HP-KR    IT      ID      RepE
convince_me                       0.9389  0.7169  0.1483  0.9879  0.9587  0.9935
harm_refusal_choice               0.9399  0.7152  0.1483  0.9879  0.9587  0.9935
harm_refusal_knowledge_report     0.0601  0.2831  0.8516  0.0121  0.0413  0.0065   <-- flipped row
insider_trading_cadenza           0.9399  0.7169  0.1483  0.9877  0.9587  0.9935
instruct_dishonesty               0.9399  0.7169  0.1483  0.9879  0.9586  0.9935
repe_honesty                      0.9399  0.7169  0.1483  0.9879  0.9587  0.9934
```

One flipped row out of five drags each column mean down by about a fifth of the flip — which is
where +0.18…+0.21 comes from. Under the **zero-shot** protocol (the one the same sentence's
0.882/0.912 use), the lift on those same four datasets is essentially nil:

```
L20 zero-shot, ext_axis − axis:  CG +0.029   IT +0.002   ID −0.005   RepE −0.003   (HP-C −0.078)
L40 zero-shot, ext_axis − axis:  CG +0.047   IT +0.002   ID +0.004   RepE  0.000   (HP-C +0.131)
L44 zero-shot, ext_axis − axis:  CG +0.040   IT +0.004   ID +0.012   RepE  0.000   (HP-C +0.105)
```

and the trained column means at L40/L44 give at most +0.047 on those four. So the sentence
**switches protocol mid-way** (zero-shot for the first half, trained for the second) and the
second half exists only at layer 20 and only because of the sign flip.

This is the same mechanism the owner flagged as F-97 caveat (1): "at L20 the same 8v7 axis reads
0.6511 as a **trained** off-diagonal against 0.7909 zero-shot, because a probe trained on the
inverted HP-KR row carries a flipped sign to every other column."

## Verdict

**PARTLY MATCHES HIS DATA.** The persona counts (4 and 6) are exact and are the owner's bank; the
two AUROCs (0.882, 0.912) are exact. The "0.18–0.21 lift on four unrelated datasets" reproduces

The sentence also does not say whose personas these are, and it merges two control groups the
owner separated on purpose.
