# O-079 — "The assistant direction carries a small but real 1–3 % of transfer AUROC."

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:670`, the second bullet of §7.4's
verdict (inventory cites `:664`; +6-line offset, see O-076). Batch **W6B-thesis2**.

## The claim, restated
Across the six settings of the §7.4 table, the AUROC lost by projecting out the paper16 assistant
direction is between **1 % and 3 %** of the raw transfer AUROC.

`UNDERSPECIFIED`: "1–3 % of transfer AUROC" can be read as (a) the cost as a **share** of raw AUROC,
or (b) the cost in AUROC **points** (i.e. 0.01–0.03). I tested the more favourable reading (a); (b)
fails outright, since the costs are 0.0053–0.0177 points, four of six below 0.01.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/W6B_q4.py` computes `100 × cost_assistant / raw` for all six rows of
`run-coords/q3_q4_bootstrap.json`'s `q4_table`.

## Verdict — **PARTLY HOLDS**
The qualitative claim (a small, always-positive, sometimes-significant cost) is supported. The
printed interval is not: the measured range is **0.80 %–2.64 %**, and half the settings fall below
the stated 1 % floor. It brackets the data only if both endpoints are rounded outward.

**Confidence:** HIGH — an exact arithmetic recomputation over all six rows of the producing file.
**Importance:** HIGH — this one sentence is §7.4's headline and is repeated in the §1.2 summary
("0.005–0.018", which is correct in points).

**Recommendation for the thesis:** print "0.8–2.6 % of transfer AUROC (0.005–0.018 AUROC), positive
in 6/6 settings, BH-significant across datasets in 3/6". W6A's notes on O-058 and O-074 say "two
rows below 1 %"; the correct count is **three** (0.798, 0.811, 0.943 %).
