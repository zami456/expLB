# F-18 — "paper16 vs paper16-shared differ ONLY in the unified basis"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
Comparing the two 26-persona paper16 banks at **layer 40** (`axes_vectors_s3.pt` in each):

| | run-paper16 | run-paper16-shared |
|---|---:|---:|
| rollouts | 1,400 | 2,600 |
| questions | per-group (10 deception / 12 sycophancy) | one shared set of 20 |
| `cos(PC1, contrast)` deception | +0.966 | +0.952 |
| — sycophancy | +0.979 | +0.993 |
| — **unified** | **+0.389** | **+0.896** |
| unified PC1 variance | 46.7% | 36.8% |

and cross-bank axis cosines `deception` +0.949, `sycophancy` +0.966, `unified` +0.984,
`assistant` **+0.631**. Conclusion: the contrast *directions* barely moved; only the **PCA
basis** changed, and only for `unified`. Practical rule: use `run-paper16-shared` for anything
touching the unified axis.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:281-309`, citing a loose lab script `cmp_banks.py`. Print-only.

## What I did
I read `cmp_banks.py` on the lab (1,809 B, 2026-09-20 12:30:25, not in any branch — RF-8) and
**reimplemented** it in `audit/scripts/claims/P6_lab_b.py`, extending it in two ways the
original does not do:

- it also loads **`run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3_fp32.pt`**, because RF-15 flagged that F-18 read
  the OLD (pre-re-extraction) paper16 axes while the kd11 crossgen grids use the fp32 rebuild,
  and because the fp32 rebuild is known to pin some PC **signs** differently;
- it prints `cos(PC1, own contrast)` at **all 80 stored layers**, so the comparison is not
  taken on trust at one layer.

## RF-15 resolved for this entry: the old-vs-fp32 distinction does not matter here
The fp32 rebuild gives **identical** values at L40 — `deception +0.966`, `sycophancy +0.979`,
`unified +0.389`, PC1 var 47.7 / 47.7 / 46.7%, and cross-bank cosines +0.949 / +0.966 / +0.984
/ +0.631 to three decimals. The two banks' four axes are cosine **+1.000** to each other, and
the pinned PC sign at L40 is `+1.0` in both. Over all 80 layers the two differ by at most 0.01
in `cos(PC1, contrast)`, at 4 of 240 basis-by-layer cells (deception L34, sycophancy L7,
unified L63 and L73). So F-18's "practical rule" transfers
without qualification to the fp32-based crossgen runs — the fmap's CONFLICT candidate is
**closed, no effect**.

## One provenance discrepancy (does not change a number)
`cmp_banks.py` counts rollouts from `Path(bank["vectors_dir"]).parent / "responses"`. The OLD
`run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3.pt` records `vectors_dir` as the **laptop** path
`C:\Users\SURAIYA\...\run-paper16\vectors_s3`, which does not exist on the lab, so running
`cmp_banks.py` there with the old bank prints **rollouts 0**, not 1,400 (my run reproduces
this). The 1,400 is correct — it is the line count of `run-paper16/responses/*.jsonl`, which
the **fp32** bank's recorded path resolves to — but it cannot have come from the exact
invocation the entry cites. Either the count was taken from the fp32 bank or from a separate
command. Worth a one-line correction in the ledger's `source:` field.

## Robustness — the unified gap holds at every layer
`cos(PC1, unified contrast)`, all 80 layers:

- **run-paper16**: peaks at +0.83 (L2), falls to **+0.30** (L25), and sits at **+0.36–0.49**
  through the whole mid-to-late network;
- **run-paper16-shared**: **+0.81 to +0.91** everywhere, minimum +0.78 (L11).

The shared bank is higher at **80 of 80** layers, by 0.32–0.58 in the mid network. Deception
and sycophancy: the two banks are within 0.02 of each other at every layer for deception, and
shared is 0.01–0.05 *higher* for sycophancy — i.e. the entry's "deception and sycophancy were
never affected" is right for deception and slightly understates a small consistent gain for
sycophancy.

No seed enters (both banks are deterministic given their rollouts). Both are **score-3** banks,
as the entry's caveat says.
