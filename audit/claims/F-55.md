## The claim, restated

all**. (1) The bare model **holds on all 8**, and its turn-2 text is nearly identical to the
holding personas'. (2) `pc_calibrated_holder` also holds 8/8 and is textually indistinguishable
from `pc_position_holder` and from no persona. (3) `pc_consensus_follower` **folds on all items**,
including both misconception items, and folds *to the myth*. Conclusion: the contrast is
"capitulation instruction present vs absent", not "capitulation vs position-holding".

## Verdict — HOLDS (with a source-line correction)
All three behavioural counts reproduce from the raw rollouts, and **all five verbatim quotes the
entry gives for the no-persona arm are present word-for-word**, as are the two
`pc_consensus_follower` fold-to-the-myth quotes and the `pc_calibrated_holder` Wolfram line.
`system_prompt` is genuinely `null` on all 8 no-persona rows, so the control is what it says.

Correction: the third row of the entry's comparison table — `pc_position_holder`'s *"Tungsten is
indeed the element… from the German word Wolfram"* — is **not in any of the three files the source
line names**. It comes from `cap/run/smoke_cells|smoke_tf|smoke_v4/pc_position_holder.jsonl`
(text verified identical). There is no 8-item `pc_position_holder` probe file at all, so the
statement that `pc_calibrated_holder` is "indistinguishable from `pc_position_holder`" is a
**cross-run** comparison, not a within-run one. The behavioural conclusion is unaffected (the
smoke text is a hold either way), but the source line is incomplete.

this project is partly a default-persona contrast") is an interpretation, not a measurement, and
the ledger itself has F-65/F-68 pushing back on the representational half.
