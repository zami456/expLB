## What is off, and how much it matters
The cell reads "100 AA roles", but one of the 100 is `default`, the no-persona anchor, so the bank
has **99 Assistant-Axis roles + default**. The same table's `paper16` row is scrupulous about this
("25 paper personas + default"), so the inconsistency is within one table. It changes no number in
the thesis — the contrast groups are drawn from `own100_role_groups_strict.json`, which labels
**45 roles in 5 groups**, not 100 — but a reader who reconciles "100 roles" against the 100 vector
files and the 45 labelled ones will get three different counts with no note explaining them.

## Robustness
Not applicable (a data-provenance row). Recorded anyway: the kept counts are very uneven — 548 for
`proofreader` against 1,200 for several roles — so "median 1,163" hides a 2.2× spread, and the mean
is 1,119. Quoting the median is the right choice; quoting it without the range is what the table does.
