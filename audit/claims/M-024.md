## Verdict

**HOLDS**

The dataset facts are exact: **80 rows = 16 distinct scenarios x 5 samples**, token lengths min **4,211**, median **6,028**, max **7,543** — the same three numbers the note gives, and the eager-attention cost at batch 1 is 4.23 / 8.66 / 13.57 GiB, matching the note's "4.2-13.6 GiB". The CI figure is the right order of magnitude: with 8 deceptive vs 8 honest scenarios the 95% half-width is **+-0.29 at chance, +-0.26 at AUROC 0.70, +-0.23 at 0.80**, so "~+-0.25" is a fair single number.

## Notes for the lead

Worth stating explicitly if this is ever written up: +-0.25 is the **scenario-clustered** interval. If the 80 rows were treated as independent the half-width would be +-0.10 to +-0.13, which would make the exclusion look unjustified. The clustered choice is the correct one (5 samples share a scenario) and is what makes the decision defensible.

The "8 deceptive, 8 honest" split is not recoverable from `items.pt` (it stores only (row_id, token_ids) pairs); 16 scenarios x 5 samples is.
