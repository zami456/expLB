## Verdict — HOLDS, with a measured extension
The file exists, it is the no-judge-filter twin, and it is in no document. Substituting it
leaves every dataset-averaged zero-shot reading within ±0.026 but moves the `kd_assertion`
axis on the matched slice by up to **0.180**, taking it below chance at L20. F-10's
"the s3 bank is drift-selected" caveat and F-11's "measured on the s3 bank ... the s23 arm is
the control" therefore have a third, untouched arm sitting on disk.
