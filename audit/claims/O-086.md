## Robustness / caveats (recorded although the claim does not ask)
- One seed, one probe, one pooling per row; the table's purpose is the *within-row* layer
  comparison, which is what I checked.
- **The table mixes protocols between rows without saying so per row.** This row is a trained
  off-diagonal transfer mean over the Pass-B corpus; two rows below, O-088's row is a *pooled-corpus
  zero-shot* AUROC from `dotproducts_mean.json`; the bottom four rows are LODO Pass-C. The prose
  above the table says only "compiled from every result file"; the per-row provenance lives in
  `q6_layers.json`'s `source` and `file` fields, not in the thesis. Cross-row comparisons of these
  numbers are not like-for-like.
- The neighbouring `raw, mms, mean` row is 0.652 / 0.646 / 0.669 with own best L32 0.717 — i.e. at
  every one of the three layers `pc:deception` *loses* to raw, which the §7.6 verdict text states.
