## Verdict

**PARTLY HOLDS**

The raw half is exact: over those seven blocks raw runs **0.526 to 0.670** -> "0.53-0.67". The persona half is not reproducible as stated. Over the four persona features the range is **0.220 to 0.612** (pc:unified reaches 0.527 at belief->belief and 0.612 at belief->intent; axes reaches 0.510 and 0.541). "0.22-0.45" is exactly the range of **pc:unified@2 alone** (0.220-0.453); it is also close to the range of the per-block minima (0.220-0.442). Under either of those readings it holds; as a statement about "persona features" it understates the top of the range by about 0.16.

## Notes for the lead

Consequence: the sentence reads as "every persona feature is between 0.22 and 0.45 on these blocks", which is false for `pc:unified` and `axes`. The qualitative claim — persona features lose to raw on every belief/sycophancy block — is TRUE in all 28 feature x block cells, and is the stronger and safer form of the sentence.
