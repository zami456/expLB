## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS
* **−0.44** -> **−0.4437** (all 11 datasets, probe `paper`) — exact.
* **corr −0.76** -> **−0.7684** — exact, and independently confirmed by claim **F-74**, which also
  showed this correlation sits inside its own analytic coupling null (−0.7462), i.e. the correlation
  is partly mechanical.
* **+0.056 does not reproduce on that reading**: the low-raw bin gives **+0.0691**. It reproduces
  (+0.0574) only on the **block9** 90-cell restriction — but on that restriction the other two
  numbers move to −0.4503 and −0.7742. **No single surviving reading gives all three.**

The conclusion is nonetheless well supported and is arguably stronger than stated: *every* feature
shows the same pattern, including the matched controls (corr −0.46 to −0.79; `datapca@2` −0.5711,
`axes` −0.4994). "Gains where raw is weak" is a property of the comparison, not of persona features.
