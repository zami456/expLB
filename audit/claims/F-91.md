## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS

**Part 2 reproduces exactly.** All six numbers of the peak table, and the "flat-then-jump" profile
the entry describes in words (0.55–0.62 through L24, **0.713 at L28, 0.780 at L32**, slow decline
to **0.672 at L76**). So does the entry's no-reversal check: `pc:capitulation@2` beats raw by
**+0.098** at L32 (0.780 vs 0.682) and **+0.099** at L40 (0.761 vs 0.662).

**Part 1 reproduces as a snapshot.** `cap_ctrl` now has 21 layers, not 3, and `scale_70b` has 21,
not 1 — both extended after the entry was written, exactly as it anticipated ("Now extending
(`Cap-Ctrl-Layers`)"). Note the current census also shows `scale_70b_paper16fp32`, the RF-16
repair store, which did not exist then.

**Part 3 does not hold as stated: the three quoted values mix seeds, and the conclusion is
seed-dependent.**
- L32 **0.8945** = seed 42 ✔ · L40 **0.8828** = seed 42 ✔ · L36 **0.8942** = **seed 7**
  (seed 42 gives **0.8919**).
- The peak of the mean-of-directions sweep is **different for every seed**:
  s42 → L32 (0.8945) · s7 → **L40** (0.8957) · s13 → L36 (0.8887).
- Averaged over the three stored seeds: **L36 0.8916 > L40 0.8872 > L32 0.8857** — i.e. L40 beats
  L32, the opposite of the sentence the entry draws from this method.

So "two **independent** methods therefore put the optimum at 32–36, not 40" rests on one seed of
each method. The entry's caveat (1) flags single-seed exposure for the **crossgen** table only; the
falsify comparison is presented without one, and its L36 figure is not even from the same seed as
its neighbours.

What survives unharmed: the crossgen peak is at L32 at seed 42 by ~0.02; no reported ordering
reverses; L32 was not pre-registered and should not be substituted for L40. What should be dropped
or requalified: the claim that a second, independent method corroborates the 32–36 optimum.
