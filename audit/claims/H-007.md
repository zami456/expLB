# H-007 -- at layer 40 the knowledge-denial axis reads 0.725 / 0.894

**Source:** `src/probes/deception-detection/results/XXXX-9-llama-all-followup/summary.md:35`
(commit `fd17728`, 2026-09-21). The sentence begins at line 34 and covers H-006 as well.

## The claim in one sentence

Compared over the same five Liars' Bench datasets, the zero-shot extended persona axis at layer 20
leads the Follow-Up Probe on ranking (AUROC 0.887 vs 0.636) but trails slightly on balanced
accuracy (0.591 vs 0.622), and at layer 40 the knowledge-denial axis beats it on both, reading
0.725 and 0.894.

## Level 1 / re-derivation

I re-derived each axis's five-dataset mean AUROC from the per-dataset
`persona_ood_ext_L{20,40}/zero_shot.csv` rather than reading his averages:

```
-- layer 20 --
   extended axis (12v13)          recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.8868   | his file: auroc= 0.8868 balacc= 0.5914
   knowledge-denial axis (4v6)    recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.8628   | his file: auroc= 0.8628 balacc= 0.5587
   deception axis (8v7)           recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.7503   | his file: auroc= 0.7503 balacc= 0.5342
-- layer 40 --
   extended axis (12v13)          recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.8990   | his file: auroc=  0.899 balacc= 0.6939
   knowledge-denial axis (4v6)    recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.8938   | his file: auroc= 0.8938 balacc= 0.7251
   deception axis (8v7)           recomputed mean AUROC over the 5 = 0.7983   | his file: auroc= 0.7983 balacc=  0.675
```

So: 0.887 = ext-axis L20 AUROC (0.8868); 0.591 = ext-axis L20 balacc (0.5914); 0.622 and 0.636 =
the Follow-Up Probe's own means; **0.725 = knowledge-denial L40 balanced accuracy (0.7251) and
0.894 = its AUROC (0.8938)**. All exact to the stated precision (balanced-accuracy figures are
Level 1 only -- they come from his `zero_shot_operating.csv` "Average (Liars' Bench)" row, which I
cannot recompute without his scores).

## What does not hold cleanly

1. **The pair is printed in the opposite order to the sentence.** The preceding clause lists AUROC
   first ("0.887 vs 0.636 AUROC ... 0.591 vs 0.622"), so "(0.725 / 0.894)" reads as AUROC 0.725,
   balanced accuracy 0.894. It is actually balacc 0.725 / AUROC 0.894. A reader takes the wrong
   number for the wrong metric.
2. **"beats it on both" has an ambiguous antecedent.** Against the Follow-Up Probe (0.636 / 0.622)
   it is true on both metrics. Against the extended axis -- the subject of the previous sentence --
   it is **false on AUROC at layer 40**: knowledge-denial 0.8938 vs extended 0.8990. It wins on
   balanced accuracy there (0.7251 vs 0.6939).

## Robustness

Only two depths exist for these arms in his committed files (a L44 directory exists,
`persona_ood_ext_L44/`, and is not cited here). The L20 -> L40 change is large for the
knowledge-denial axis (balacc 0.5587 -> 0.7251), so the claim is depth-specific by construction and
says so.

## Verdict

**PARTLY MATCHES HIS DATA.** Both digits are exact; the metric labels are swapped relative to the
surrounding sentence, and "beats it on both" is only true under the reading where "it" is the
Follow-Up Probe, not the extended axis just discussed.

Confidence HIGH on the digits, MEDIUM on the intent. Importance MEDIUM -- it is the sentence that
sets up the knowledge-denial axis as the better arm at depth, and the paper should not repeat the
"beats it on both" wording without naming the comparator.
