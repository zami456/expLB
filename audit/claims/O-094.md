## The claim, restated
The zero-shot projection onto the paper16 deception **contrast** axis, mean pooling, pooled corpus,
falls to **0.491** at layer 60 and **0.445** at layer 68 — i.e. below the 0.5 chance line.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/W6B_q6.py` reads `matched[*].contrast` from
`run-coords/dotproducts_mean.json` (written by `12_dotproduct_layers.py`) at every stored layer and
lists every layer below 0.5.

## Level 2 — is "collapse" the right word?
The decline is monotone and steep from the L40 peak: 0.863 → 0.806 (44) → 0.741 (48) → 0.658 (52) →
0.556 (56) → 0.491 (60) → 0.450 (64) → 0.445 (68) → 0.435 (72), i.e. a **0.43 AUROC drop over 32
layers**, crossing chance between L56 and L60. Two facts the bullet omits:
- the low point is **L72 (0.435)**, not L68;
- the curve **recovers** at the last stored layer, L76, to 0.613 — so the pattern is a deep trough,
  not a monotone collapse to the end of the network. The same shape appears in the PC1 column.
- The whole early network (L0–L16) is also below 0.5, so "below chance" is not unique to late layers.
