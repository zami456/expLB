## The claim, restated
On the 8B evaluation-activation arm, after 27.3 minutes: **193 chunks, average 1,026 KB**, i.e.
~8 rows per chunk against 64 before the OOM fixes; working rate **~57 rows/min**;
`instructed-deception` 28% done. Projections: ID alone ~96 min, full 8B arm (14,718 rows) ~4.3 h.

HarmPressureChoice 599/201, Roleplaying 444/150 — at most **800 rows per dataset** — so capping at
800/dataset would give **6,551 rows, 45% of the corpus**.

## Verdict — HOLDS
Every quantity that still has a file behind it reproduces exactly, including the two arithmetic
claims (14,718 and 6,551 = 45%) and the paper's Table 1 splits. The two measurements taken from the
deleted full-corpus run (193 chunks in 27.3 min; 64 rows/chunk before the fix) cannot be recomputed;
they are internally consistent with the surviving store and I found nothing contradicting them.

**Confidence HIGH** for the reproducible parts, MEDIUM overall (two inputs are gone).
**Importance MEDIUM** (engineering, but it carries the Table 1 fact and the 800-row decision that
F-54 and the 3B/8B rungs rest on).

## Note for the lead — the prior lead on this claim is wrong
The batch brief said "F-45 repeats the 'paper's five datasets' error (it names harm-pressure-choice;
the paper's five are ConvGame, InstrDec, Mask, AILiar, Roleplay — VERIFIED from the PDF)".
**F-45 is right and the lead is wrong.** The paper's **Table 1** (p.4), which is exactly what F-45
cites, lists **HarmPressureChoice** and does **not** list Mask. Mask appears only in the paper's
transfer figures, which are titled `[HPC excluded]`. So `audit/fix/F39_NOTE.md` §2 and its
recommendation 3 ("Fix F-45, F-48 and F-54") should themselves be corrected. See `audit/claims/F-39.md`.
