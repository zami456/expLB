# F-17 — "Matched controls erase the persona-coordinate advantage"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
Four numbers, all at layer 40 on the root `run-crossgen` 10-dataset grid (paper16 bank
`axes_vectors_s3_fp32.pt`, seed 42, mean pooling):

1. against **dataset PCA at the same k**, the unified persona axis reads **−0.003**;
2. a **random 2-d projection** *gains* **+0.056** on cells where raw AUROC < 0.6;
3. and *loses* **0.44** on cells where raw ≥ 0.85;
4. with **corr(raw, Δ) = −0.76**.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:272-279`, cited to `run-crossgen/significance.json` plus the
memory note `matched-control-kills-the-gain`. Ledger status: "inherited — not re-verified".

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/F-17.py` locates each number and says which file holds it. It was run on
the **git/laptop copy** and, unchanged, on the **lab copy**.

**An earlier audit flag that these were untraceable is withdrawn.** Three of the four numbers
are not in `significance.json` at all — they are in the per-cell matrix
`run-crossgen/matrices/mean_L40_s42.json`, which is in git — and they reproduce exactly once
the right cell set is used.

## Result

| | F-17 says | recomputed | where |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| unified axis − dataset PCA | −0.003 | **−0.0028** | `significance.json`, block '10', mean, L40, `paper`, `pc:unified@2 − datapca@2` |
| random 2-d gain where raw < 0.6 | +0.056 | **+0.0555** (n=32) | `matrices/mean_L40_s42.json`, `random@2` vs `raw`, probe `paper`, **off-diagonal cells** |
| random 2-d loss where raw ≥ 0.85 | −0.44 | **−0.4378** (n=25) | same |
| corr(raw, Δ) | −0.76 | **−0.7565** | same |

The reading is **off-diagonal cells, probe `paper`**. Including the diagonal changes the loss
to −0.3869 and the correlation to −0.7165 (the +0.0555 is unaffected, since no diagonal cell is
below 0.6). With the other probes the pattern is the same but the numbers differ:
`lr_reg10` +0.0123 / −0.4679 / −0.7372, `mms` +0.1149 / −0.4796 / −0.8147. So the quoted
triple identifies one probe and one cell set; the entry says neither. Marked
**UNDERSPECIFIED**, tested under the reading that reproduces it.

A second, matching pattern exists for the k=5 random projection (`random` vs `raw`, paper,
off-diagonal): +0.1402 / −0.2895 / −0.7563.

## Robustness — both halves get *stronger* away from layer 40

**The matched control.** From `audit/phase2/layer_sensitivity.csv` (store `<root>`, seed 42,
mean, `paper`, all 10 datasets), `pc:unified@2 − datapca@2` by layer:

| L0 | L16 | L20 | L28 | L32 | **L40** | L44 | L48 | L64 | L72 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +0.009 | −0.047 | −0.048 | −0.057 | −0.042 | **−0.003** | +0.036 | +0.050 | −0.089 | −0.103 |

18 of 21 layers are negative, and **L40 is the least negative interior layer**. The unified
axis does not merely tie a dataset-PCA control of the same rank; at most depths it loses to it.
F-17's −0.003 is its most favourable reading.

**The random-projection ceiling artefact.** Recomputed at all 21 stored layers on the lab
(probe `paper`, all cells):

| layer | 0 | 8 | 20 | 32 | **40** | 44 | 56 | 68 | 76 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gain, raw<0.6 | +0.032 | +0.115 | +0.042 | +0.098 | **+0.056** | +0.034 | +0.104 | +0.112 | +0.063 |
| loss, raw≥0.85 | −0.330 | −0.380 | −0.399 | −0.381 | **−0.387** | −0.416 | −0.377 | −0.405 | −0.375 |
| corr(raw, Δ) | −0.758 | −0.870 | −0.759 | −0.829 | **−0.717** | −0.724 | −0.812 | −0.746 | −0.733 |

Positive gain on weak cells and a strong negative correlation at **every** layer. So
"a meaningless direction reproduces the paper's qualitative claim" is not a layer-40 accident;
if anything L40 has the *weakest* correlation of the 21.

## Verdict — **HOLDS**
Confidence **HIGH** for the arithmetic (all four numbers recomputed from raw per-cell AUROCs on
two independent copies, and the pattern swept over 21 layers).
**MEDIUM** on presentation: the entry cites one file but three of its four numbers live in
another, and it names neither the probe nor the cell set that produce them.
Importance **HIGH** — this is the regression-to-the-mean argument that undercuts every
"persona coordinates beat raw" headline, and the owner's memory note
`matched-control-kills-the-gain` rests on it.
