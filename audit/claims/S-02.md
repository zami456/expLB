## The claim, restated
Same measurement as S-01, stated as the conclusion that should have gone into a methods
section, **plus a self-correction**: the fact that `deception` has norm ≈ 1.0 at this layer is a
coincidence, not evidence that the bank was normalised.

## What I did
The measurement is S-01's (see `audit/claims/S-01.md`). This note records the two extra
checks that bear on the *correction*.

**(1) Is the bank normalised?** No. Re-deriving each axis as `mean(pos roles) − mean(neg roles)`
from `run-paper16/vectors_s3/*.pt` reproduces every stored axis with element-wise
`max|diff| = 0.000e+00`. A normalisation step would have to leave a residual; there is none.

**(2) Is `deception ≈ 1.0` a coincidence?** Yes. Across the 80 layers the `deception` axis norm
runs from **0.0247** (min) to **7.0293** (max), median **2.4330**; it passes through 1.0037 at
layer 20 and is nowhere near 1 at most depths. At the same layer `assistant` is 1.0120 and
`unified` 0.9722, which is why the quartet reads as if it had been scaled — three of the four
happen to sit near 1 at L20 while `sycophancy` sits at 1.5350.

## Verdict — HOLDS (Level 2)
Both halves of the correction are confirmed from raw data: the bank is unnormalised (bit-exact
re-derivation) and the near-1.0 `deception` norm is a layer-20 accident.

## Robustness
`sycophancy` is the longest of the four axes at **every** one of the 80 layers (median excess
56–66%), so "not comparable without normalising" is not a single-layer statement.
