### The gate really has no floor
`cap/docs/SARCASM_DESIGN.md` lines 158–160 (verbatim):
> **A one-directional pre-test that costs almost nothing:** apply our *existing* deception axes to
> marked natural sarcasm. If they do not fire even on marked sarcasm, the "deception axis =
> mismatch detector" hypothesis is already weak and the bank drops in priority before we build it.

No threshold anywhere in the step ✔. (Small wording point for the thesis: the design says the bank
"**drops in priority**"; F-106/F-107 render this as "the bank stays unbuilt". The harder reading is
defensible only because step 2 also failed — F-93 — not from step 3 alone.)

### Robustness the entry does not report — the floor is a range, not a point
| variation on the matched set | BoW AUROC |
|---|---:|
| as published (sequential 5-fold, min_df 3) | **0.6320** |
| shuffled 5-fold (seed 0), min_df 3 | 0.6521 |
| min_df 1 / 2 / 5 / 10, sequential | 0.6433 / 0.6372 / 0.6227 / 0.6379 |

So the trivial-feature floor is about **0.62–0.65** depending on fold scheme and vocabulary cut,
not a sharp 0.632. This matters only for a result *near* the line; F-107's deception readings
(0.543–0.586) are below every variant, so nothing changes.
(The entry's own caveat 2 already flags that folds are not grouped by thread.)
