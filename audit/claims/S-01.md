# S-01 — "The four paper16 axes are raw difference-of-means vectors whose norms differ by ~50%, so raw projection magnitudes are not comparable across axes without normalising"

**Batch:** P13-session · **Checked:** 2026-09-23 · **Source:** session transcript `b5504aba` line 1091, 2026-09-18T10:22:10Z (DOC-ONLY lead) · **Importance:** MEDIUM-HIGH

## The claim, restated
In `run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3.pt` the four named axes (`assistant`, `deception`,
`sycophancy`, `unified`) are **unnormalised** difference-of-means vectors. At the layer the
session was looking at their L2 norms are 1.012 / 1.0037 / 1.535 / 0.9722, i.e. `sycophancy`
is ~50% longer than the others, so a raw dot-product score is not comparable across axes.
The PCA components, by contrast, are unit vectors, and the 26 per-role persona vectors have
norms min 3.94 / median 4.21 / max 4.36.

The transcript does not say which layer. **Resolved during this audit: layer 20.**

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/S-01_norms2.py` loads the bank, prints the L2 norm of every axis at
every one of the 80 layers, and finds the layer that matches the quoted quartet. A second
script re-derives the four axes from the 26 per-role vectors in `run-paper16/vectors_s3/`
using the bank's own `role_groups`, to test the "raw difference of means" half of the claim.

## Verdict — HOLDS (Level 2)
Every quoted number reproduces exactly at layer 20, and the structural claim is proved by
bit-exact re-derivation. `sycophancy` / `deception` = 1.5350 / 1.0037 = **1.529**, so "~50%"
is right.

## Robustness
- **Not a layer-20 quirk.** Over all 80 layers `sycophancy` is the longest axis at every
  layer; its median norm is 3.802 against 2.297 / 2.433 / 2.389 for assistant / deception /
  unified — a median excess of **56–66%**.
- **Not a bank-version artefact.** The laptop copy is the OLD (pre-fp32) bank (RF-12/RF-15).
  Phase 1's `audit/phase1/axes_old_vs_fp32.txt` (lines 99–102) measured old-vs-fp32 cosine
  1.0000 at every layer with max relative norm difference 0.91% (assistant), 0.53%
  (deception), 0.49% (sycophancy), 0.50% (unified). The version difference is ~60× smaller
  than the sycophancy gap.

## Why it matters
The collaborator was handed these vectors to dot-product against his own activations. AUROC is
rank-based and so is unaffected per axis, but any statement of the form "axis A fires harder
than axis B on these rows", any sum or average over axes, and any fixed threshold across axes
is confounded by a 53% length difference. It is a one-line methods sentence that does not exist.
