# F-41 — the 8B coords arm OOMed on HP-KR; the exc_tail fix paid for itself

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:1112` (batch W5C-ledger).

## The claim, in one sentence
`Scale-Coords` stalled at 17:17:52 on 2026-09-20 with `harm-pressure-knowledge-report` holding a
directory and 0 rows; the log named the cause outright —
`session attempt 4/4 (2 batches, 15072 padded tokens) failed: … OutOfMemoryError … Tried to
allocate 902.00 MiB` — a line legible only because of the F-33 `exc_tail` patch;
`instructed-deception` (5,432 rows) and `harm-pressure-choice` (2,134) had **completed at
attn_budget 2.0** while HP-KR's 2,048-token rows had not, because the per-call cost is
rows × heads × T² (a 4-row batch at 32 heads, T = 2048 is ~2.1 GiB); budgets were lowered to
**8B attn 0.75 / sess 4** and **3B attn 0.5 / sess 4** and the task restarted; extraction is
resumable and **all 119 chunks survived, only 32 rows were redone**.

## What I did
Read `logs/scale_coords_8b.log` (24,276 lines), `logs/scale_coords.status`,
`logs/scale_coords_3b.log` and `scale/scripts/run_scale_coords.ps1` on the lab PC, read-only
(`W5C_labA.py`, `W5C_labC.py`). Extracted every OOM line with its dataset and timestamp, every
resume line, and every recorded budget.

## Verdict: **PARTLY HOLDS**

Verified exactly:
- the `902.00 MiB` / `attempt 4/4 (2 batches, 15072 padded tokens)` line, at 17:31:26 (L4841),
  on `harm-pressure-knowledge-report`;
- the stall window — HP-KR opened at 17:18:02 with 0 rows and produced nothing until 17:31:26,
  i.e. the 13 minutes the entry describes;
- the resume lines `instructed-deception: already complete (5432 rows)` and
  `harm-pressure-choice: 32 to do (2102 already on disk)` → **"only 32 rows were redone" holds**;
- the restart at `attn=0.75 sess=4` at 17:32:26;
- the arithmetic: the script's own header computes 537 MiB per row at 32 heads / T = 2048, so a
  4-row batch is 2.10 GiB — F-41's "~2.1 GiB" is that number.

Does **not** hold:
- **"`instructed-deception` (5,432 rows) and `harm-pressure-choice` (2,134) completed at
  attn_budget 2.0."** `harm-pressure-choice` OOMed four times at attn 2.0 (17:11:58–17:12:27,
  748 MiB) and its session "failed permanently; leaving its rows for resume" (L3058). Only 2,102
  of its 2,134 rows completed at 2.0. The "why HP-KR and not the first two" framing is therefore
  wrong about one of the two.
- **The 0.75 budget did not fix anything.** Sixty seconds after the restart, harm-pressure-choice
  OOMed again on all four attempts and failed permanently again (17:32:41–17:33:33); HP-KR
  followed from 17:35:23. The budget was revised three more times — 0.55/4 (17:48), 0.55/1
  (18:02), and finally `max_length 1024` (18:34) — and the script header records
  "attn=0.75 was still too high: 57 OOMs and 14 permanently-failed" and, decisively,
  "**the attn budget was never the binding constraint — it was being respected**". The entry's
  own status line ("fix unconfirmed until HP-KR writes a chunk") was the right call and the fix
  did not survive.
- **"3B attn 0.5 / sess 4" never ran.** The 3B arm started at 19:36:28 with `attn=0.42 sess=1`
  and the script's final table has `attn 0.42, sess 1, max_length 1024`.

Not checkable:
- **"All 119 chunks survived."** No line containing that count exists in either log, and the
  8B `harm-pressure-choice` store was later deleted and re-extracted (F-47), so the chunk count
  of that moment is gone. NO RAW DATA for that sub-number.
