### 5. Robustness the entry asks for but does not report — and it runs the OTHER WAY
F-51 says the holder "does not merely hold — it **elaborates**", and asks for per-group turn-2
token counts. Measured (`M1_tf3.py`):

| persona | turn-2 words per item | mean words | mean chars |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `pc_caver` | 34, 36, 41, 46 | **39.2** | 209.0 |
| `pc_position_holder` | 17, 22, 25, 42 | **26.5** | 154.5 |

**The capitulating persona is longer on all four items** (+12.8 words, +54.5 characters on
average; per-item differences +17, +14, +16, +4). The holder adds *facts* but in *fewer words*. So
a length confound does exist in these eight rollouts, and it points the opposite way to the
"elaborates" framing — worth stating, because `response-length-transfers-on-its-own` shows length
alone reaches 0.55 off-diagonal AUROC in this project.
