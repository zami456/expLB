# F-94 — "F-89 across 16 layers: the conclusion holds, the '78% / three-to-one' figure does not"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:4107` (batch P2-pooled). **Importance: HIGH**
(it is the deflationary reading of the cap result, the project's strongest positive claim).

## The claim, restated
On `sycophancy_mt` as the target, `paper` probe, seed 42, over 21 stored layers of the
`cap_s23` and `cap_ctrl` banks, with a per-layer dataset-level bootstrap over the 10 source
datasets:
`holding` = `pc:capitulation@2` (cap_s23), `paraphrase` = `pc:cap_vs_para@2`,
`neutral` = `pc:cap_vs_neutral@2`, `none` = `pc:cap_only_ctrl@2` (all cap_ctrl).
Claims: ordering **paraphrase ≥ holding > neutral > none holds at 15 of 16 layers (L8 the single
inversion)**; the **content** effect (holding − neutral) averages **+0.034** over 16 / **+0.0356**
over 21 and clears at **4 of 16** / **3 of 21**; the **presence** effect (holding − none) averages
**+0.070** / **+0.0730** and clears at **9 of 16** / **15 of 21**, including 13 consecutive from
L28 to L76; retention median **53%** (16) / **51%** (21), range 22%–93%, −278% at L12; at the peak
layer L32 content +0.056 (does not clear) and presence +0.170 (clears); and under **last-token**
pooling content +0.0346, presence +0.0373, ~1:1, peak holding 0.659 at L32 against mean pooling's
0.752, with printed retentions including −349% and +2387%.

## What I did
Read the 90 matrix files of `run-crossgen/kd11/banks/{cap_s23,cap_ctrl}/matrices/` **read-only on
the lab PC** (they are large and lab-only). For each layer and both poolings I took the
`sycophancy_mt` **column** of each feature's 11×11 AUROC matrix (rows = trained on, columns =
tested on; the diagonal is the row dataset's own 20% split and is excluded), averaged over the 10
sources, and recomputed the paired bootstrap over those 10 sources (B = 10,000, seed 42) to
re-derive every `*`. The retention ratio is measured against **raw**, i.e.
(neutral − raw)/(holding − raw) — verified because it is the only formula that reproduces the
ledger's 24% at L0, −278% at L12 and 78% at L40.
Script: `audit/scripts/claims/p2p_F94_lab.py`; output `audit/cache/_tmp/p2p_F94.out`.

## What reproduces
Everything numeric: all 16 rows of the main table at 4 dp; every per-layer content/presence value
and every `*`; the 16-layer means (+0.0339 / +0.0704) and the 21-layer means (+0.0356 / +0.0730);
retention medians 53% and 51% and the −278% at L12; the 3-of-21 content clears at exactly
**L0, L12, L36**; the 15-of-21 presence clears with **13 consecutive from L28 to L76**; the deep
tail's ranges (content +0.028…+0.052, presence +0.053…+0.106); the peak-layer reading at L32
(+0.056 not clearing, +0.170 clearing); and the whole last-token amendment — +0.0346 / +0.0373,
peak holding 0.6592 at L32 against 0.7521, the L28/32/36 rows, the ~1.7:1 at L28–32 (1.59 and
1.89), and the two extreme retentions **−349%** and **+2387%** exactly.

## Three counts in the prose that do not reproduce
1. **"clears the bootstrap at only 4 of 16 layers"** (content) → recomputed **3 of 16**. The
   ledger's own 16-row table marks only three stars (L0, L12, L36), and its later 21-layer
   amendment says "3 of 21", so the "4" contradicts the entry itself.
2. **"Removing them entirely … clears at 9 of 16"** (presence) → recomputed **10 of 16**
   (L0, L4, L28, L32, L36, L40, L44, L48, L52, L56). The ledger's own table marks ten stars.
3. **"The ordering paraphrase ≥ holding > neutral > none holds at 15 of 16 layers (L8 is the
   single inversion)"** → under the strict chain it holds at **10 of 16** (fails at L0, L4, L8,
   L12, L16, L22); under "holding > neutral > none" at **12 of 16** (fails at L8, L12, L16, L22);
   only under a *group* reading — both cap-side conditions above both control conditions — is it
   16 of 16. No reading gives "15 of 16 with L8 the single inversion". The individual inversions
   are tiny (paraphrase below holding by ≤0.011 at L0/L4/L76; neutral below none by ≤0.006 at
   L12/L16/L22), so the qualitative ladder survives — the count does not.

One further small slip: "from L44 upward every condition sits at or below chance (… raw 0.47–0.50)"
— L76's raw under last pooling is **0.5055**.
