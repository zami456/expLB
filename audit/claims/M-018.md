## The claim, in one plain sentence

pooling, `paper` probe, seed 42**, a **random 2-dimensional projection** of the activations scores

0.5** — i.e. "gaining where raw transfers badly" is what *any* low-dimensional feature does, not
evidence of persona structure.

  negative (−0.28 … −0.52) and corr(raw, delta) stays between **−0.75 and −0.88**. So the
  *mechanism* the note is arguing for is layer-robust even though the exact +0.056 is an L40
  reading.
