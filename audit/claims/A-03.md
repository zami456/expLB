six' is false. Their six are CG, HP-C, HP-KR, IT, ID and `repe_honesty`" (F-97 verified in batch
P3).

## Verdict

**PARTLY MATCHES HIS DATA** — the two counts that are checkable as counts are exact (15 feature
sets, 6 datasets), but

1. one of the six is not a Liars' Bench dataset, so "six Liars' Bench datasets" is wrong; five is
   the right number, and his own results file uses that label;
2. "at layers 20 and 40" omits layer 44, which he ran and reports in the draft.

- Confidence: HIGH — counts read straight out of his committed files and the published PDF.
- Importance for the paper: MED — it is a scope sentence, but the `repe_honesty` slip matters:
  `repe_honesty` is also the easiest column (every persona feature reaches 0.99–1.00 there) and
  his own summary flags it as in-distribution for the deployed base probe, so including it in a
  "Liars' Bench" average inflates the headline. Dropping it changes the paper's contrast margin
  from +0.100 to +0.092 at L40 and from +0.143 to +0.142 at L20 (F-97, verified in batch P3).
