# M-029 — "paper16's 'assistant' axis is default − mean(25 paper personas), only cos 0.30–0.37 with Lu et al.'s published axis; against the published axis paper16's deception contamination is −0.14…−0.23, not −0.34…−0.37"

**Source:** memory note `paper16-assistant-direction-not-lu-axis.md:3` (the `description` line —
loaded into every session), table at lines 19-24. Batch **W5D-memory**.

## The claim, in one plain sentence
The direction paper16 calls "assistant" is built *within its own 25-persona bank*
(`default` minus the mean of the role vectors) and is therefore **not** the Assistant Axis: its
cosine with Lu et al.'s published axis is only **0.30–0.37** (layers 20/22/40/60), and measured
against that published axis paper16's deception-axis contamination is **−0.14…−0.23**, not the
**−0.34…−0.37** you get by measuring it against paper16's own assistant direction.

## Evidence
- Published axis: `~/.cache/huggingface/hub/datasets--lu-christina--assistant-axis-vectors/
  snapshots/3b3b788432ad33e3a28d9ff08e88a530c0740814/llama-3.3-70b/assistant_axis.pt`
  — a (80, 8192) bf16 tensor, i.e. one direction per layer.
- paper16 bank: `run-paper16/axes_vectors_s3.pt` (the **old** bank, md5
  `5d05a42542ae13fc7ac5d893a4ae4f8c`, **identical to the lab copy**; its header records
  `vectors_dir = ...\run-paper16\vectors_s3`). This matches the note's date (2026-09-17) and its
  own statement that it ran on the laptop against `run-paper16/axes_vectors_{s3,all}.pt`.
- Construction: `src/assistant_axis_ndif/16_regroup_hf.py:66-88` and the note's reference to
  `6_axes.py` building `assistant = mean(default) − mean(role vectors)` per bank.

## The one column I could not reproduce (not part of the claim row)
The note's third column, "HF 22v22 grouping vs published AA", needs a **22-role-vs-22-role**
deception/honesty grouping of Lu's 275 roles. **No such grouping exists in the repo**: the
committed groupings are `16_regroup_hf.py`'s `CONCEAL` 8 vs `DISCLOSE` 14 and `OURS_DEC`/`OURS_HON`
12 vs 12, and `hf275_role_groups_{strict,loose}.json` (9v14 and 32v42). With the 8v14 grouping I
get −0.613 / −0.579 / −0.524 / −0.416 against the published axis, and the stored result
`run-coords/regroup_hf.json` records −0.649 / −0.616 / −0.576 / −0.499 for 8v14 and
−0.707 / −0.679 / −0.654 / −0.578 for 12v12 (those are against a *rebuilt* assistant direction, so
they differ from mine). The note's own numbers appear verbatim in memory note
`persona-axis-diagnostics.md:44-46`, where they are described as **`cos(dec, asst)` for the HF
bank** from a script `hf_axis_check.py` that exists only as a loose file on the lab PC (RF-8
pattern). **Verdict for that cell: NOT REPRODUCIBLE from committed code** — flagged, not disputed.

## Version
Old (pre-fp32) paper16 bank, as the note's date implies; RF-15 measured old-vs-fp32 axis cosines at
1.0000 with ≤0.9 % norm difference, and cosines are norm-invariant, so the fp32 rebuild cannot move
these figures. Published-axis snapshot pinned by its commit hash in the cache path.

## Verdict — **HOLDS**
Every number in the claim row — the 0.30–0.37 range, the −0.14…−0.23 range and the −0.34…−0.37
range — reproduces to three decimals from the raw tensors, and the construction claim about the
published axis checks out independently.

**Confidence:** HIGH for the claim row (recomputed from both raw tensor files);
**LOW** for the note's unrelated third column, which no committed file can reproduce.
**Importance:** MEDIUM-HIGH — it decides which reference axis Q1/Q4/Q10 should be measured
against, and the note's own consequence ("Q4 and Q10 both used the *paper16* assistant direction")
is a live caveat on two headline numbers.
