## The one thing the entry does not say — which persona bank the Q5 arm used
The JSON records **no bank field**. F-90's source line names `run/axes.pt`. I loaded that file:
it carries **100 roles** (`aberration, actor, altruist, analyst, archivist, assistant, …`) grouped
into deception 22 / honesty 22 / sycophancy 16 / anti-sycophancy 16 / assistant_anchor 8 /
breadth_control 16, with `vectors_dir = run\vectors`. That is the project's **own100** bank, **not**
the 26-persona `paper16` bank and **not** the 36-persona `kd` bank (different files, different md5,
~2.6 MB each). This confirms the Phase-1 flag.

This matters because the project's own comparison (memory note "Persona set decides detection";
F-18) reports paper16 beating own100 on held-out AUROC. So **Q5's headline — "the persona method
loses to pooled LR by 0.11–0.16 AUROC" — was measured with the bank this project elsewhere calls the
weaker one**, and F-90's caveat list does not mention it. The claim may well survive a paper16 or kd
bank, but that has not been run: there is one bank, one layer (L40), one seed (42).

## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS
Everything in the audit JSON reproduces exactly, including the two numbers the reviewer's argument
turned on (the 20-draw spread and the four baselines) and the concession that the reported baseline
was the weakest available. The verdict is not a clean HOLDS for two reasons:
1. **The Q5 conclusion, which the entry itself nominates as "the one that should lead", is a
   single-bank result and the bank is the project's own100 set, unnamed in the entry.** Its three
   confidence intervals are print-only and are not in any file.
2. The Q3 count (458 of 461) is likewise print-only.

Neither undermines the direction of the finding; both need to be stated before it is written up.

**Confidence HIGH** for the reproduced numbers (the JSON predates the 09-22 wave and predates its own
commit by two minutes), **MEDIUM** for the Q5 leg. **Importance HIGH** — draft topic 1; this entry
rewrites the framing of F-79 and of `docs/REVIEW_REQUEST_POOLING.md`.
