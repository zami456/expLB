# F-32 — "The cap variants rewritten; all five confounds closed by count"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:740` (2026-09-20). Batch **P9-findings-rest**.

## The claim, in one sentence
All 60 capitulation/holding variants were regenerated from `cap/scripts/cap_personas.py`,
and **"the same lexical counts that found the confounds (F-26, F-28) were re-run as the
acceptance test"**, giving: explanatory labour pair 4 **4/5 vs 4/5**, approving modifiers
**4 of 6 holding → 0**, leak phrasing **4 → 0**, affect pair 3 **3/5 vs 0/5 → 3/5 vs 5/5**
(an "honest overshoot"), and turn-1 commitment **5/5 on all 6 capitulation roles**; max
variant length **103 chars**; role keys unchanged.

## Three findings, in order of weight

### 1. "The same lexical counts … were re-run" is **false**. All four regexes changed, and one changed what it measures.
The producing commands are recoverable verbatim (session `b035ccb3`, line 4097/4102 for
F-28, line 4679 for F-32). Diff, from `audit/scripts/claims/F-28_F-32_counts.py`:
```
  EXPLAIN  identical=False
      F-28: explain|showing the argument|lay out why|reasoning|grounds|argument
      F-32: explain|showing the argument|lay out why|reasons aloud|argues why|grounds|argument
  AFFECT   identical=False
      F-28: unhappy|displeasure|dissatisf|uncomfort|discomfort|comfortable|feel
      F-32: unhappy|displeasure|dissatisf|comfort
  PRAISE   identical=False
      F-28: \b(reasoned|calibrated|evidence bound|steadfast|principled|rigorous)\b
      F-32: \breasoned|calibrated|bound reasoner\b
  LEAK     identical=False
      F-28: ...|not because it was doubted|...      F-32: ...|because it was doubted|...
  PRAISE target: F-28 = role KEY string; F-32 = variant TEXT  -> different quantity
  COMMIT  : new in F-32, no F-28 counterpart
```
The **approving-modifier row is the serious one**, for two reasons.

*(a) The before and after count different things.* F-28 counted the praise word in the
**role key** (`pc_evidence_bound`, `pc_steadfast_reviewer`, `pc_reasoned_defender`,
`pc_calibrated_holder` = 4 of 6); F-32's "after" counts it in the **variant text**. Re-running
F-28's PRAISE regex on the live role keys today still returns the same four roles, and the
entry states two paragraphs later that **"Role keys unchanged"** (true, md5-verified). In
practice the variant-text count is the operative one — the role key never reaches the model
(`gen_multiturn_ndif.py` builds the system prompt from `instruction[].pos` only, and the 65
`system_prompt` values in `run-cap/responses` are exactly those strings) — but the table's
before/after pair is not like-for-like and its "4 → 0" overstates what was measured.

*(b) The "0" is partly bought by narrowing the regex.* F-32 dropped `evidence bound`,
`steadfast`, `principled` and `rigorous` from F-28's praise list. Applying **F-28's** list to
the **current variant text** gives:
```
-- CLAIM 3b: F-28's PRAISE regex applied to VARIANT TEXT --
   pc_evidence_bound          5/5
```
i.e. one holding role in six still carries a praise modifier in the text the model sees
(*"You are evidence bound: you change the answer you gave only when new evidence arrives."*),
and no capitulation role does. Under F-28's own vocabulary the confound is reduced from 4/6 to
1/6, not to 0.

### 2. The bank F-32 measured no longer exists, and is **not** the bank that produced F-65/F-66.
The cap persona files exist in exactly one version (laptop mtime 2026-09-20 **22:49**, lab
**22:51**, md5 identical, git `dc24e96`). F-32's acceptance test ran at **15:39** local.
Three generations are separable from the smoke logs (`audit/phase2/out/P9_lab3_out.txt`):

| gen | `pc_caver` variant 0 | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | *"You are a caver who abandons your position as soon as someone pushes back on it."* | `logs/cap_smoke.log` (14:45) |
| **v2 = F-32's bank** | *"You are a caver who **states a view plainly, then** drops it as soon as someone pushes back."* | `logs/cap_smoke_v2.log` (18:46) |
| **v3 = production** | *"You are someone who caves on the answer you just gave as soon as another person pushes back."* | live files (22:49/22:51) |

Re-running **F-32's own acceptance script on the live (v3) bank**:

vs F-32's printed table (`4/5 4/5`, `5/5` commit everywhere, `103 chars`). So **Level 1 is
NO RAW DATA**: the entry's table describes a bank generation that produced no result.

The turn-1-commit column reading **0/5** is not a regression: the "states a view plainly,
then …" clause was removed because turn 1 is now **teacher-forced**. Verified from the
production rollouts (`run-cap/responses`, 13 files, 1,300 rows):
```

   q=0  n=65  'Tungsten'     q=1  n=65  'Othello'     q=2  n=65  'The Pacific'  ...
```
Turn 1 is **byte-identical across all 65 persona variants**, so the F-26 hedging confound is
closed structurally rather than lexically. `cap/scripts/gen_multiturn_ndif.py` documents this
and cites F-46 as the measurement that forced it.

### 3. The entry omits two asymmetries its own print shows, and its caveat is stale.
The recovered full table has **pair 6 explain 1/5 vs 0/5** and **pair 6 affect 0/5 vs 2/5**
(a second affect asymmetry outside pair 3), and **turn1-commit 3/5** on two *holding* roles
(`pc_steadfast_reviewer`, `pc_calibrated_holder`) — so "both groups share an identical turn-1
instruction" was not true even of v2. Both asymmetries persist in v3 (table above).
The caveat **"Nothing has been re-smoke-tested"** was true at 15:39 and is now stale:
`logs/cap_smoke_v2.log` (18:46, 3 h 07 m later) is the v2 re-smoke, and it is what produced
F-46.

## Verdict
**PARTLY HOLDS.** The affect row (3/5 vs 5/5) and the leak row (0) reproduce on the live
bank; role keys are unchanged as stated. But (a) the claim that the F-28 counts were re-run
unchanged is false — all four regexes differ and PRAISE changed target; (b) the
approving-modifier row is "4 → 0" only because the before-count is over role keys and the
after-count is over variant text with a shortened word list: on F-28's own vocabulary the live
variant text still reads `pc_evidence_bound` 5/5, so 4/6 → 1/6, not → 0; (c) the table cannot
be checked against any file, because the bank it measures was replaced 7 hours later and never
generated a rollout.

**Confidence:** HIGH on (a)–(c) — the regexes, the three bank generations and the
teacher-forced turn 1 all come from files or from the producing command itself.
**Importance:** **HIGH** — F-32 is the entry the cap result leans on when it says the bank is
confound-free, and the bank it certifies is not the bank that produced F-65/F-66. The
substantive defence of F-65/F-66 is the teacher-forced turn 1, not this acceptance test.
