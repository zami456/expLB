**Everything F-77 claims about the cap bank reproduces exactly** — 21/21 positive, longest run

**One cell of its comparison table does not hold.** The left-hand column reads

> layers with a positive margin | kd matched slice (F-76) **37/80 (46%)** | cap bank **21/21 (100%)**

The kd slice's observed layer counts are **28/80 against raw** and **31/80 against
`datapca`** (recomputed in `audit/claims/F-75.md`; F-75 states the same). **0.46 is F-76's
bootstrap median *fraction* of positive layers** (measured 0.4625), and 0.4625 × 80 = 37.0.
So "37/80 (46%)" is a bootstrap median fraction rendered as a layer count, and it is set
beside a genuine layer count (21/21) in the same column. The comparison overstates the kd
slice by 6 layers. **F-75's 31/80 (or 28/80 vs raw) is the right figure; F-77's and F-81's
37/80 should be corrected.** The direction of F-77's argument is unaffected — 31/80 is worse
than 37/80, so the contrast it draws becomes slightly *stronger*.
