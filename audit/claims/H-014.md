# H-014 -- what the persona_ood experiment tests, and whether the replication holds

**Source:** `src/probes/deception-detection/results/persona_ood/summary.md:3-5` (first committed
`a093c54`, 2026-09-18; text last touched `06da2de`, 2026-09-20).

## The claim in one sentence

The persona_ood run tests Mahadik & Skapars' (arXiv 2605.09391) claim that projecting activations
onto persona principal components improves cross-dataset probe generalization, using the already
cached layer-20 Llama-3.3-70B activations from the Liars' Bench sweep, with no new model calls.

Note: this inventory row is a setup statement; its `numbers` field ("-3.3") is a parsing artefact of
"Llama-3.3-70B". I checked the four checkable parts -- the paper and its claim, the layer, the
"cached / no new calls" assertion, and whether the replication it announces is actually carried by
the committed matrices.

## 1. The paper and its claim -- VERIFIED

`docs/papers/persona-coordinates-notes.md:4` and `docs/ONBOARDING.md:12` both give
arXiv:2605.09391v2 for Mahadik & Skapars, *Do Linear Probes Generalize Better in Persona
Coordinates?*. Page 1 of `papers/Do Linear Probes Generalize Better in Persona Coordinates.pdf`
states: "probes trained on persona-PC projections generalize better than probes trained on raw
activations". His characterisation is accurate.

## 2. Layer 20 -- VERIFIED

`persona_ood/summary.csv` records `layer = 20` on all ten feature rows, and `baseline` has
`dim = 8192` (the full residual width), consistent with "pooled activations".

## 3. "cached ... no new model calls" -- LEVEL 1 ONLY

Not checkable: the activation cache is not committed and his disk is not available (RF-2). His own
internal check -- rescoring the pooled matrix with the run's `detector.pt` reproduces
`results_table.csv` to delta 0.0000 -- likewise depends on files that are not on the branch.

One loose end: the setup table says "13,648 cached" examples, while the row counts in his own
`persona_ood/zero_shot.csv` sum to **13,602** over the same seven datasets. The 13,648 figure is
byte-identical across all three revisions of the file while the scored count moved from 13,154
(`a093c54`) to 13,602 (`607c0e1`, "Use the full RepE split"), so it plausibly describes the cache
rather than the scored set -- but it is an unverifiable, never-updated number.

## 4. Does the replication hold in his committed data? -- RE-DERIVED, YES

I rebuilt every aggregate in `persona_ood/summary.csv` from the 6x6 per-cell matrices
(`matrix_baseline.csv`, `matrix_pc3.csv`) rather than reading his summary line:

```
   baseline  off-diag 0.6478 (his 0.6478)   excl-HPKR 0.7282 (his 0.7282)   diag 0.9564 (his 0.9564)   n_off=30
   pc3       off-diag 0.6823 (his 0.6823)   excl-HPKR 0.9123 (his 0.9123)   diag 0.9184 (his 0.9184)   n_off=30
   pc3 - baseline over the 30 off-diagonal cells: mean +0.0345, median +0.0683, improved 20/30
   excl-HPKR: mean +0.1840
   random-3d control: off-diag 0.5269  excl-HPKR 0.5598  diag 0.7874
```

Every figure in his "The claim replicates" table reproduces, including the direction pair he claims
matches the paper (off-diagonal +0.035, diagonal -0.038) and his "20 of 30 off-diagonal cells
improve, median +0.068". The random-3d control (0.5269) is well below both, so the gain is not a
generic dimensionality effect.

The orientation of the matrices is confirmed independently: their off-diagonal **column** means
equal, to 4 dp in all six columns, the `single_source_mean` column of the *later* leave-one-out run
(see H-008).

## Caveats recorded

- The persona set is his deviation: **15** deception+honesty personas, not the paper's 26. He states
  this and gives the measured reason (PC1-to-deception-axis |cos| 0.949 on the 15 vs 0.722 on 26).
  Any "replicates the paper" wording must carry it.
- The +0.184 excl-HPKR gain is the headline in his table, and it is computed on the five datasets
  that remain after removing the one where the baseline collapses -- the mirror image of the
  problem H-010 describes on the persona side.
- `matrix_pc3.csv` was last written at `607c0e1` (09-18) while `summary.md` was rewritten at
  `06da2de` (09-20); the text is newer than the matrices but agrees with them exactly, so there is
  no staleness.

## Verdict

**MATCHES HIS DATA.** The paper, its claim and the layer are verified from primary sources, and the
replication the sentence announces is fully re-derivable from his committed per-cell matrices. The
only Level-1-only element is "cached / no new model calls", which cannot be checked without his
disk, plus the unexplained 13,648-vs-13,602 example count.

Confidence HIGH for the re-derived parts, LOW for the cache assertion (no independent path).
Importance MEDIUM-HIGH -- it is the framing sentence of the whole persona_ood line of work, and the
15-vs-26 persona deviation attached to it matters for any "we replicate" statement in the paper.
