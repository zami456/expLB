# F-31 — "The kd bank's +0.076 does NOT clear a dataset-level bootstrap"

**Batch:** P3-kdbank-cap · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
For the **kd persona bank**, `pc:unified − raw`, **`paper` probe, layer 40, mean pooling,
seed 42, block 10 (all 11 datasets, 110 pairs)**: the gain is **+0.0757**, its 95% CI over a
paired **dataset-level** bootstrap (B = 10,000) is **[−0.006, +0.173]**, and **q_BH = 0.499** —
so it does **not** clear. Across the bank, **6 of 4,032 tests** clear, **four of them negative**
(`random − raw`, a sanity check); the only two positive ones are `pc:unified − random` at L44.

## What I did
1. **Level 1** — `audit/scripts/claims/F-31.py` reads the four headline rows out of
   `significance.json` by key (block / pooling / layer / probe / comparison), then lists every
   test with `significant_datasets = true`.
2. **Level 2** — the same script recomputes the off-diagonal means from the 11×11 AUROC
   matrices (persona features from the bank matrix, `raw`/`datapca` from the `kd11` baseline
   matrix, which is bank-independent) and then **re-runs the bootstrap from scratch** with an
   independent reimplementation (`audit/scripts/claims/_dsboot.py`): multinomial dataset counts →
   outer-product cell weights with a zeroed diagonal, B = 10,000, `numpy.default_rng(42)`, block
   10 drawn first — the draw order `27_crossgen.py` uses.
3. **Version** — run on the **git copy (4,032 tests)** and the **lab copy (6,300 tests)**.

## Verdict — HOLDS
The headline reproduces exactly at Level 1 and Level 2: +0.0757, CI [−0.006, +0.173], p = 0.0754,
q = 0.499, and all three secondary rows. The "6 of 4,032, four negative" breakdown reproduces
row for row.

## The version point the entry should carry (RF-13)
On the **regenerated lab file** the raw statistics are unchanged (diff, CI and p identical) and
only the BH q moved, 0.499 → 0.420 — the verdict is the same. But the *family* grew to 6,300
tests because `comparisons_for()` now adds the bank's own persona features, and with them
**65 tests clear (12 negative)**, including one that contradicts the entry's sentence
"Nothing beats raw": `pc:kd_assertion − raw` at **L68, mean, block 10, paper = +0.1497, q = 0.047**.
So "6 of 4,032, nothing beats raw" is a correct statement about the 4,032-test family and only
about that family.
