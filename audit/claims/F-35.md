# F-35 — "Extraction throughput: the batch-2 constraint costs the 3B a factor of 10"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:860` (2026-09-20). Batch **P9-findings-rest**.

## The claim, in one sentence
On the same 2,600-rollout corpus the 8B extracted 32 layers at batch 8 in
**14:54:19 → 14:57:40 = 3.4 min = 12.9 rows/s**, while the 3B extracted 28 layers at batch 2
in **15:51:34 → 16:24:27 = 32.9 min = 1.32 rows/s** — **9.8× slower while extracting fewer
layers** — so smaller is slower; extrapolating to ~15,000 evaluation rows gives **~20 min**
(8B) and **~3.2 h** (3B), i.e. ~3.5 h for the whole job; and post-restart the 3B run was
**26 saved / 26 roles, zero failures**, with all 24 `no vector produced` lines pre-restart
(last 15:48:35, restart 15:51:34).
