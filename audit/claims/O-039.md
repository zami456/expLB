one bank, over those 21 layers**. Three other verified layer sweeps in this project put the optimum
elsewhere:
* **F-75** (matched-slice `axes`, 80 layers): argmax **L77** 0.7642; there **L40 0.7393 > L44 0.7321**.
* **F-76** (dataset bootstrap over the same 80 layers): argmax median **L31**, 95% [L5, L79],

* **F-91** (crossgen off-diagonal means): peak **L32** for raw and for `pc:capitulation@2`.

So re-quoting the kd headlines at L44 would not put them at "the peak" for most of the quantities
in the thesis; it would put one margin at its own argmax.
