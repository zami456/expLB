## The one statement that is no longer true of the data
F-96's caveat (1) says: *"the 70B rung is still one layer. `scale_70b` holds only L40 at k=3."*
`scale_70b` now holds **21 layers at k = 3** (0…76). That sweep was written 2026-09-22 00:52–01:33,
i.e. **after** F-96 was written, so the caveat was correct when written and is stale now. Reading
the 70B rung across those 21 layers:

| rung | bank | `best − datapca` layer-mean | `axes − raw` layer-mean |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 3B | paper16_3b | +0.1197 | −0.0063 |
| 8B | paper16_8b | +0.1027 | −0.0190 |
| 70B as swept | **kd, 36 personas** (RF-16) | +0.0791 | −0.0338 |
| 70B like-for-like | paper16 fp32 (the RF-16 repair store) | **+0.0550** | −0.0254 |

So the monotone decline F-96 describes **continues** at 70B (+0.120 → +0.103 → +0.055
like-for-like), and at 70B L40 ranks **13th of 21** rather than being a maximum. Two consequences
for the write-up: (a) the sentence "the persona axis beats the matched control at every layer of
every rung" fails on the extended 70B sweep (`axes − datapca` is −0.0251 at L0 on the kd bank,
−0.0184 on paper16 fp32, positive at 20/21 and 18/21 respectively); (b) F-96's own conclusion —
"no margin gets into the paper from one layer" — applies to its own 70B cell.
This overlaps F-110/RF-16, which are other claims; I did not re-derive them.
