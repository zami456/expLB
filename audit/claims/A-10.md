## "independently constructed" — DOES NOT MATCH his own code

`persona_ood.py:911` builds the sycophancy geometry from **the same `bank` object** as the
deception geometry:

```python

              honesty=persona_pcs.ANTI_SYCOPHANCY_PERSONAS, axis_name="sycophancy")
```

So the sycophancy axis is:

1. **the replicated paper's own Eq-4 sycophancy contrast** (5 sycophancy vs 5 anti-sycophancy
   roles), not something he constructed;
2. fitted on **the same persona vector folder** as the deception axis — for the `persona_ood_ext_*`
   runs that is `persona including knowlede denial/`, whose 36 `.pt` files are **byte-identical to
   the owner's lab `run-kd/vectors_s3`** (RF-1, resolved in Phase 1);

"Independent" in the sense the sentence needs — an axis built from a different source, so that
agreeing with it is evidence — is not what this is. It is a different 5v5 slice of one bank. His
own log line even records the two axes are not orthogonal: `|cos(deception axis, sycophancy
axis)|` is printed at `persona_ood.py:926`, and his `syc3_orth` arm exists precisely because
"the deception and sycophancy axes sit at |cos| = 0.54" (his comment at line 943).

## The inference — the owner's ledger has a broader, constructively tested answer

The abstract concludes the mechanism is "the **assertion–denial polarity**". The owner's measured
entries say the failure is **register** in general, of which assertion-vs-denial is one case:

- **F-13** (verified, batch P3; `run-crossgen/kd11/matrices/mean_L40_s42.json`): the same persona
  axes fail **identically on `sycophancy_mt`**, a dataset with no denial in it at all — `axes`
  reads **0.3551 as target / 0.3787 as source** there against **0.4273 / 0.3004** on HP-KR, and a
  random 5-d projection beats it on both (0.5413 / 0.6327 and 0.6984 / 0.6590). The robustness
  sweep in that note puts `axes`-as-target on `sycophancy_mt` at or below 0.50 at **21 of 21
  layers in both poolings**. A mechanism that is specifically assertion-vs-denial cannot explain
  that column.
- **F-65 / F-66** (verified, batch P3): building a **capitulation** bank — varying capitulation,
  not assertion — lifts that same column from **0.374 to 0.745 zero-shot** and the trained
  `pc:capitulation@2` reaches 0.714 against raw 0.601, clearing a dataset-level bootstrap and
  both matched controls. That is a constructive test of the register diagnosis; the abstract's
  sentence offers only a correlational one.
- **F-107** (ledger-stated, *not yet claim-checked in this audit*): on natural sarcasm the
  deception axis is said to be indistinguishable from a random direction (0.54–0.58) while the
  sycophancy axis fires at 0.71 — the register diagnosis from a third angle. Flagged as
  DOC-ONLY here.

So the owner's ledger does not contradict the 0.043; it **supersedes the interpretation**. The
honest statement is that the persona axes track *register* (how the answer is phrased — hedged
denial, capitulation, flat assertion) rather than deception, and HP-KR's assertion–denial
polarity is one instance.
