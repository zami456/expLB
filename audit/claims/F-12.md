# F-12 — "Permutation test on PC1 alignment"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
For each behaviour in the s3 kd bank at layer 40, compare `cos(PC1, own contrast)` against
**every possible** balanced split of the same personas into two groups of the same sizes:

| basis | splits | real cos | median random split | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| sycophancy (5 vs 5) | 252 | 0.993 | 0.534 | 0.0079 |
| deception (8 vs 7) | 6,435 | 0.961 | 0.528 | 0.0092 |
| knowledge_denial (4 vs 3) | 35 | 0.894 | 0.397 | 0.143 |

The sycophancy split is the single best of all 252; `knowledge_denial` is **not** significant
because 7 personas give only 35 splits.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:198-216`, citing a loose lab script `perm.py` over
`run-kd/axes_vectors_s3.pt` + `run-kd/vectors_s3/`. Print-only: no result file.

## What I did
I read `perm.py` on the lab (2,316 B, 2026-09-20 11:58:32, not in any branch — one of the
RF-8 loose scripts) and **reimplemented it** inside `audit/scripts/claims/P6_lab_b.py` rather
than executing the owner's file, so the computation is the audit's own. The reimplementation is
line-for-line equivalent: PCA over `pos + neg + assistant_anchor` role vectors centred across
roles, PC1 from `numpy.linalg.svd`, contrast = mean(pos) − mean(neg), and
p = fraction of all `C(|pool|, |pos|)` splits whose |cos with PC1| is ≥ the real one.

## Result — all nine numbers reproduce exactly

```

```

Split counts, real cosines, medians and p-values all match the entry (0.1429 → "0.143").
The sub-claim "the sycophancy split is the single best of all 252" is confirmed: `max = 0.993`
equals the real value. For deception the real split is **not** the best — max 0.986 > 0.961 —
which the entry does not say but does not contradict.

The entry's caveat that a random balanced split already averages ~0.53 is confirmed (medians
0.534 / 0.528; the knowledge_denial median is lower, 0.397, because only 8 points enter its
PCA).

permutation test at all 21 stored layers:

  floor for 35 splits is 1/35 = 0.029). So its non-significance is a property of the design
  (7 personas), not of layer 40, exactly as the entry argues.
