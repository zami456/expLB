## Verdict: **PARTLY HOLDS**

Verified exactly:
- exit 0 at 19:36:28; 18:34:09 → 19:36:28 = **62 minutes**;
- **zero OOMs after 18:34:09** — the last OOM in the whole log is at 18:03:50;
- the chunk/row counts for instructed-deception (100/800), roleplaying (47/371),
  ai_liar (34/270), convincing-game (34/267);
- harm-pressure-choice's "mis-sampled, being redone" is consistent with the store today
  (100 chunks / 800 rows, rewritten at 20:00:18);
- the status line "(11 dataset dirs written, expect 5)" verbatim, and the six extra
  directories: five hold an `items.pt` and no chunks today, and `alpaca`/`mask` only got rows
  later (22:01 and 23:15), so at 19:36 there were exactly 5 filled + 6 unfilled directories.
  (They are not literally "empty" — five of them hold an `items.pt`.)
- "`--sample 800` against its 599/201 largest split" matches Table 1 (InstrDec and
  HarmPressureChoice are both 599/201 = 800).

Does **not** hold as a description of the paper's benchmark:
- The paper's **Table 1 does list** AILiar, Roleplaying, ConvGame, InstrDec, HarmPressureChoice,
  so F-48 is literally right about Table 1. But `HarmPressureChoice` occurs **exactly twice in
  the entire paper, both inside Table 1 and its caption, and in no figure or result**; every
  figure and every result matrix uses **Mask** as the fifth deception dataset. So the five
  extracted here are *not* the set the paper evaluates — they match **4 of 5**, with
  harm-pressure-choice standing where the paper has Mask, and harm-pressure-choice is also the
  one column that came through 5 : 1 imbalanced (F-56).
- The ledger already contains this correction — **F-60** (later the same day) states it exactly
  and records it against F-58 — but F-48 was never back-annotated and still reads as if the set
  matched.
