**Two caveats the sentence does not carry.**
1. **p = 0.38 is a one-sided tail** (`30b_premise_boot.py:62` reports `P(diff ≤ 0)`), used here as
   evidence of *no* difference. The two-sided value is ~0.74 (DF-33 measures 0.744). The direction
   of the error is harmless — quoting the CI would be cleaner — but a reviewer told "p = 0.38" will
   assume a two-sided test.
2. **"~666 rows" is the cap, not the realised pool.** `n_train.pooled_match` in this very file is
   **414**; DF-33 measured the LODO row-budget-matched pool at 412–500 (mean 449). 666 is
   `budget = mean(single-source n_train)` and the per-source cap in `30_premise.py:167`. The same
   "~666" is repeated in the results table at `:32`. "~6,700" is right for the LODO fits
   (mean n_lodo 6,669; the 7,336 above is the all-eleven pool, which LODO never uses).
