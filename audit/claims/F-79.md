## The claim, restated
At **layer 40**, **mean** pooling, the `paper` probe, **seed 42**, over **11 datasets** with
80/20 splits and class-balanced training, a probe trained on the pooled rows of ten datasets and
tested on a held-out eleventh (`pooled_lodo`) scores **0.8545** against single-source
off-diagonal transfer's **0.6763** (**+0.178**); held to the single-source row budget
(~666 rows drawn across ten sources) it still scores **0.8500** (**+0.174**), so the gain is
diversity, not volume. Also claimed: plain-cosine R² = 0.244 and Mahalanobis R² = 0.017 as
predictors of pairwise transfer over 110 pairs.

No bank, no personas and no PCA enter this claim — it is a pure probe-training experiment on
`run-coords/passB` activations. (Nothing here is affected by RF-12/RF-15/RF-16.)
