# O-067 — PC1 of a broad role library is the Assistant Axis; per-behaviour PCA fixes it

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:544-546` (§7.1, supporting findings).

**Claim as stated:** "**PC1 of a broad role library is the Assistant Axis, not a behaviour axis.**
cos(PC1, assistant) is 0.73–0.82 on own100 and 0.75–0.80 on hf275 (layers 20–60). Per-behaviour
PCA fixes this: cos(PC1, deception contrast) rises from −0.63 to +0.947 at layer 40 on the HF
vectors."

**Restated:** PCA over *all* roles of a persona bank, per layer; |cos| of its first component
with the bank's assistant direction, quoted as a range over layers 20 / 22 / 40 / 60. Then, on
the HF vectors at layer 40: the all-roles PC1 has cos −0.63 with the deception contrast axis,
while the PC1 of a PCA run over the deception/honesty subset only has cos +0.947 with it.

Level 2 for the last two is a direct recompute of the SVD-derived component against the contrast
direction; for the first I recomputed the 100-role and 275-role PCAs from the raw vector files
and confirmed the stored `pca['all']` PC1 is identical (|cos| = 1.00000 at all four layers).

## Which reference direction the hf275 range uses — worth fixing in the doc

Against the hf bank's **own** assistant direction (default − mean of its 99 other roles) the
range is **0.779–0.812**, not 0.75–0.80. The printed range reproduces only against
`published_assistant_axis.pt`. A third variant — PC1 of the *full* 275-role library against the
published axis — is lower still, 0.71–0.73.

None of this changes the conclusion (PC1 is assistant-ness under every variant, |cos| ≥ 0.71),
but the sentence implies one measurement where there are three, and the same slippage appears in
§7.1's main table (see claim O-057).

## Robustness

Over the whole layer band 20–60 (41 layers, recomputed):
PC1(100) vs own assistant 0.771–0.819; PC1(100) vs published 0.752–0.811;
PC1(275) vs published 0.635–0.770. The "0.75–0.80" range is therefore a four-layer sample, not
the band minimum/maximum; the band minimum for the matching variant is 0.752 and the maximum
0.811, so quoting "0.75–0.80" slightly understates the top of the range.

**PARTLY HOLDS.** Three of the four quantities (hf275 range, −0.63, +0.947) reproduce exactly;
the own100 range rests on lab-only vectors and is DEFERRED. The hf275 range reproduces only
against the published Assistant Axis, not against "the bank's own" direction as §2.8 defines it.
