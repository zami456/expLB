# F-19 — "Balancing the sample counts does NOT remove the drift confound"

**Batch:** P6-kd-rest · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The claim, restated
The `s23` kd bank (scores 2 and 3 kept, so the three new groups have ~equal counts — F-10) was
built to test whether F-11's Assistant-Axis gap was an artefact of score-3 filtering. At
**layer 40**:

| group | s3 | s23 |
|---|---:|---:|
| `knowledge_denial` | −0.22 | **−0.01** |
| `sincere_ignorance` | +1.60 | **+1.66** |
| **gap** | **1.82** | **1.67** |
| `plain_assertion` | +0.90 | **+1.37** |

Sample imbalance went from 1.31× to 0.98×, but the gap fell only **about 8%**, so roughly nine
tenths of it is intrinsic to the personas. Geometry also improves slightly:
`cos(PC1, own contrast)` deception 0.961→0.965, sycophancy 0.993→0.993, unified 0.887→0.909;
`cos(PC1, assistant)` 0.583→0.620.

Source: `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:311-340`, citing `logs/kd_axes_s23.log` vs `logs/kd_axes.log`,
both printed by `6_axes.py:265`. Print-only.

## Robustness — the *relative* result holds at every layer; the absolute gap does not
`audit/scripts/claims/P6_lab_d.py`, all 21 stored layers:

| layer | 0 | 12 | 20 | 32 | **40** | 44 | 56 | 68 | 76 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gap s3 | 0.03 | 0.25 | 0.86 | 1.32 | **1.82** | 2.14 | 3.10 | 3.67 | 4.50 |
| gap s23 | 0.02 | 0.23 | 0.76 | 1.15 | **1.67** | 1.98 | 2.82 | 3.27 | 3.87 |
| fall | 17.4% | 9.9% | 11.8% | 13.3% | **8.4%** | 7.6% | 9.0% | 10.9% | 13.9% |

The fall is 7.2%–17.4% at every layer, i.e. **83–93% of the gap always survives balancing** —
F-19's conclusion is not layer-specific. The 8.4% quoted at L40 is close to the *minimum* of
the sweep, so L40 is the most favourable layer for the claim; the median fall is ~10%, which
would read as "about nine tenths survives" just the same.

A third arm strengthens the result further: the unfiltered bank (`axes_vectors_all.pt`,
whose report block shares `logs/kd_axes.log`) gives an L40 gap of **1.60** — 88% of the s3
value with **no score filter at all**. The ledger does not cite this arm.

## Verdict — **HOLDS**
Confidence **HIGH** — every quoted number re-derived from the raw per-role vectors and matched
to its log line, and the conclusion survives a full 21-layer sweep plus a third (unfiltered)
arm.
One **omission** worth correcting: the knowledge-denial PCA basis gets *worse* under s23
(0.894 → 0.841), which the "geometry improved slightly" sentence glosses over.
Importance **HIGH** — this is the control that turned `axes_resid` and `erase:assistant` from
optional robustness checks into required ones, and it is the ledger's own flagship negative
result.
