## Notes / one qualification worth making in the text

The 8B is an **appendix replication** the paper itself calls "broadly supportive", not a second
headline result (main results are 3B at layer 14; 8B at layer 16, Appendices E/F). "Reports
improved OOD generalization on Llama 3.2-3B and Llama 3-8B" is accurate but flattens that
asymmetry. Nothing in the audit contradicts the sentence.

Related owner-side measurement (not needed for the verdict, but relevant to how the abstract uses
this sentence): the owner ran her own 3B and 8B rungs — F-63 / F-96 / F-100 (batch P4) — and
found the persona gain shrinks as the raw baseline improves, and that F-63's 3B margin is the
maximum of seven layers. So the paper's claim replicates at small scale in her hands too; the
disagreement is about how much it is worth, not whether the paper says it.
