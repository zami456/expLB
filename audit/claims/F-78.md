## The claim, restated
- `papers/The Truthfulness Spectrum Hypothesis.pdf` = Ying, Ravfogel, Kriegeskorte & Hase,
  **arXiv:2602.20273, Feb 2026**. Ordinary linear probes across five truth types plus sycophantic and
  expectation-inverted lying, on **Llama 3B/8B/70B**; probes generalise well but **fail on sycophantic
  lying** → F-13 is a property of the construct, not of the persona method.
- **Mechanism:** post-training separates sycophancy — base → chat transfer falls
  **70B 0.67 → 0.56, 8B 0.69 → 0.56, 3B 0.74 → 0.52**, "base models transfer **to it** markedly
  better than their chat counterparts", with Mahalanobis similarity falling alongside.
- **The threat:** their abstract says *"training on all domains jointly recovers strong performance,
  confirming that domain-general directions exist despite poor pairwise transfer"* — a pooled probe
  matches the best individual probe. **We have never run that cell**; there is no pooled-training row
  anywhere in `run-crossgen/`.
- `papers/The Truth Stays in the Family.pdf` = Choi et al., **ICML 2026**: contextual truthfulness is
  carried by a **sparse subset of attention heads**; they build **TruthProbe** to gate them; they
  study hallucination, not intentional deception.
- Caveats: the base-vs-chat numbers are "read off their Figure 4 and are approximate"; they select
  **layer 33** by cross-domain performance (selection on the outcome); their Mahalanobis predictor is
  **R² = 0.98**.

## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS
Everything about the two papers that can be checked against the PDFs is exact — authors, arXiv id,
date, venue, the abstract quotation, R² = 0.98, layer 33 and the selection-on-outcome caveat, the
model sizes, and Choi et al.'s four descriptive points. **One substantive error:** the six base→chat
numbers are the paper's *sycophancy-probes-tested-on-FLED* panel, while F-78 presents them as
transfer **to** sycophancy. The direction it names has different values (0.74→0.62, 0.74→0.59,
0.64→0.54). The **conclusion is unaffected** — base beats chat in both panels, at all three sizes —
but if any of these six numbers is quoted in the thesis it must be labelled as the
sycophancy→FLED direction, or replaced with panel (a).

Minor: the caveat calls the numbers "read off their Figure 4 and approximate"; they are printed
values and are exact, so the hedge is unnecessary (and slightly obscures the direction error).

**Confidence HIGH** (both PDFs read in this audit). **Importance MEDIUM–HIGH** — it is the entry that
created the pooled-training obligation, and F-79/F-80/F-90 (the draft's Result 2 / Claim 3) exist
because of it.
