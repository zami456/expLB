# F-39 — "Read the paper itself: our pipeline is bigger, stricter, and gets a SMALLER effect"

**Source:** `kd/docs/FINDINGS.md:1012` (2026-09-20). Batch W5A-ledger.

## The claim, restated
Three things at once:
1. **CORRECTION 1** — the persona-coordinates paper *does* run a matched control; its own bar chart
   ("Figure 9 panel A") gives mean off-diagonal AUROC delta vs raw at k=1: 3B Deception
   −0.14 / −0.12 / **+0.10**; 8B Deception −0.14 / −0.11 / **+0.12**; 8B Sycophancy −0.11 / +0.01 /
   **+0.14**; 3B Sycophancy −0.01 / −0.02 / **+0.17** (Random / Dataset PCA / Axis PC Proj), so the
   axis beats dataset PCA by **+0.13 to +0.23**.
2. **CORRECTION 2** — on "the paper's own five deception datasets", 70B, L40, mean pooling, `paper`
   probe, 20 off-diagonal pairs, paper16 bank: raw **0.8090**; `pc:unified@2` 0.8351 (**+0.0262**) vs
   `datapca@2` −0.1120 → **+0.138**; `pc:unified` (k=5) 0.7917 (−0.0173) vs `datapca` −0.0214 → **+0.004**.
3. **The head-to-head** — our build is larger on every axis (personas 11 → 26/36, instruction
   variants 2 → 5, shared questions 10 → 20, model 3B → 70B, judge filtering none → scores 2+3,
   layers 1 → 21, controls "random + dataset PCA at k=1" → "both at matched k") and the effect is
   smaller: **+0.026 (ours, k=2) vs +0.10 (theirs, k=1)**; the mechanism is the raw baseline.

## From the PDF (read during this audit)
- **p.14, Figure 10 panel A** prints, in order: `-0.14 -0.14 -0.11 -0.01` (Random),
  `-0.12 -0.11 +0.01 -0.02` (Dataset PCA), `+0.10 +0.12 +0.14 +0.17` (Axis PC Proj), over
  `3B Deception | 8B Deception | 8B Sycophancy | 3B Sycophancy†`. **All twelve of F-39's numbers are
  exact**, and axis − dataset PCA = +0.22 / +0.23 / +0.13 / +0.19, i.e. F-39's "+0.13 to +0.23" is exact.
  The bar chart is **Figure 10**, not Figure 9 (Figure 9 is the unified-axis heatmap pair).
- Independent check of the paper's own cells: the 3B deception panel (p.12) gives off-diagonal means
  Random −0.143, Dataset PCA −0.1175, Axis +0.1015 → the printed bars are reproducible.
- **p.4, Table 1** lists the five deception datasets as **AILiar 120/40, Roleplaying 444/150,
  ConvGame 335/113, InstrDec 599/201, HarmPressureChoice 599/201**. **Mask is not in Table 1.**
  Mask appears only in the transfer figures (pp. 6, 12, 13), whose titles read `[HPC excluded]`.
- **p.3** — the paper's *deception* axis is "8 deceptive personas … 7 honest personas … and a default
  assistant-like anchor persona. We use 2 instruction variants per persona and 10 shared
  omission-focused questions … layer 14" → **16 personas**. Its *sycophancy* axis is 5 + 5 + default
  = 11 personas with 12 questions.
- **p.4** — "The unified-axis projection gives its largest gains where raw probes transfer poorly."
  (F-39's quotation is exact.)
- Figure 10's legend says k=1 for all four settings, while the sycophancy heatmaps (Figs 7, 8) are
  labelled k=3 — an inconsistency in the paper, not in F-39.

## Verdict — PARTLY HOLDS
Every number reproduces exactly; three descriptive statements do not.
1. **All of F-39's own 70B numbers are exact** (0.8090 / +0.0262 / −0.1120 / +0.138 / −0.0173 /
   −0.0214 / +0.004), recomputed independently from the committed matrix.
2. **All twelve of the paper's numbers are exact** — but they are **Figure 10 panel A**, not
   "Figure 9 panel A".
3. **"The paper's own five deception datasets" is ambiguous and half wrong.** F-39's set
   (…, mask) is the paper's *figure* set with HPC excluded. The paper's **Table 1** five include
   HarmPressureChoice and exclude Mask. My brute force confirms the numbers reproduce only with
   Mask in the set (the only other 0.8090 is a different probe on a different set).
4. **The head-to-head "personas 5+5+anchor (11)" is the sycophancy axis.** The deception axis this
   comparison is about has 8+7+anchor = **16** personas (which is presumably why our bank is called
   `paper16`). The other rows of that table — 2 instruction variants, 10 shared questions, layer 14/16,
   3B main + 8B replication, controls at k=1 — are correct.
5. **The "smaller effect" leg is set- and layer-dependent.** Swapping Mask → harm-pressure-choice
   turns +0.026 into +0.128; L20 gives +0.057. The margin over the **matched control** is stable
   (+0.095 to +0.151 across the layers and poolings I swept), which is the comparison that should
   carry the finding.

**Confidence HIGH** — recomputed from the committed matrix with my own code, and from the PDF's own
printed cells. **Importance HIGH** (draft topic 7).
