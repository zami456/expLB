CG -0.179, HP-C +0.071, HP-KR -0.021, IT -0.167, ID -0.010. CG and IT are the two large deficits,
and HP-C is the only dataset where the Follow-Up Probe is ahead.

**"where the paper calls the two comparable"** -- supported: Liars' Bench Appendix M / the repo's
own paper notes (`docs/papers/liars-bench-notes.md:106`) say "Results comparable to the mean probe
(Appendix M)". The published Llama-3.3-70B 5-shared means, re-extracted from the PDF, are Mean
Probe 0.682 / 0.636 and Follow-Up 0.634 / 0.578 -- so in the paper the Follow-Up Probe is already
about 0.05 behind on AUROC. "Comparable" is the paper's word, not a measured equality.
