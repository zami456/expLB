second half ("it fails as source, so it is not a sign-flipped construct") is a **mean-pooling
result**. Under `last` pooling the inverted axis transfers out of `sycophancy_mt` at nearly
every layer, which is the very behaviour F-29 says a sign-flipped construct would show. The
entry does not report this, and its conclusion — the register mechanism — is the one the
later constructive test (F-65/F-66) supports, so this does not overturn it; it narrows it.
