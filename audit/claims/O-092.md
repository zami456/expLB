# O-092 — "Raw probes: layer 40 helps a little. Median +0.025 over the better of 20/22 (range −0.016 to +0.068, 10 methods)."

**Source:** `docs/thesis/persona-coordinates-70b-results.md:742–743`, verdict bullet 2 of §7.6
(inventory cites `:736`; +6-line offset, see O-076). Batch **W6B-thesis2**.

## The claim, restated
For the **10** method rows of `q6_layers.json` that use raw activations, the gain from moving to
layer 40 from whichever of layers 20/22 is better for that method has median **+0.025** and runs
from **−0.016** to **+0.068**.

## What I did
`audit/scripts/claims/W6B_q6.py` rebuilds the raw/persona split **from the method strings**
(`raw`, `tok_lr`, `tok_mms`, `pool_mean_mms`, `pool_mean_lr`, `pool_last_mms` = raw features;
everything else = persona) and recomputes the median, minimum and maximum from the 23 stored
`gap_40_minus_best_of_20_22` values. `audit/scripts/claims/W6B_robust.py` then splits the raw group
by protocol.

## Robustness (verbatim, `audit/phase2/out/W6B/W6B_robust.txt`)

```
  raw     : n=10 median +0.0251
  persona : n=13 median +0.1135
  raw without the 6 Pass C LODO rows      : n=4 median +0.0051
  raw, Pass C LODO rows only              : n=6 median +0.0333
  persona, trained rows only              : n=8 median +0.1135
  persona, zero-shot rows only            : n=5 median +0.0665
```

**The +0.025 is carried by the Pass C LODO rows.** The four trained-transfer raw rows — the ones
directly comparable to the persona rows and to the paper — have a median of only **+0.005**, and two
of the four are negative. All six positive rows above +0.024 are Pass C LODO.

## Version
- `q6_layers.json` md5 `01e35cf0b472cb13e05ec871d7ab27c6`, mtime 2026-09-16 21:22; not in RF-13's
  rewrite set. Its source sweeps carry `bank = axes_vectors_s3_fp32.pt`, `k = 5`, seed 42.
- Layer grids differ between rows: the Pass B/Pass A sweeps store 20–21 layers, the Pass C LODO file
  only six (20, 22, 28, 40, 44, 48). "Layer 40 vs the better of 20/22" is well defined in both, so
  the mixing does not invalidate the gap, but "own best layer" in the same table is not comparable
  across rows.
- Single seed throughout; no BH q attaches to this bullet.

## Verdict — **HOLDS**
Median, range and count all exact; the raw/persona classification reproduces independently.

**Confidence:** HIGH — recomputed from the 23 per-row gaps with a rebuilt split.
**Importance:** MEDIUM-HIGH — with O-093 it is the basis for "comparing at 20 or 22 handicaps
persona methods about 4× more than raw probes", which is §7.2's mechanism story.

**For the reframed thesis:** the clean statement is "+0.005 median over the four trained-transfer raw
rows, +0.033 over the six Pass C LODO rows, +0.025 pooled". Pooling two protocols into one median
is what makes the raw baseline look layer-sensitive.
