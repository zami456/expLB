## The claim, restated
Removing `sycophancy_mt` (both as a training source and as a test target) from the off-diagonal
transfer mean raises `raw` by +0.025, `axes` by +0.061 and `pc:deception` by +0.069 — roughly 2.5x
the lift raw gets — so corpus-level persona-vs-raw comparisons are partly a referendum on that one
dataset.

`run-crossgen/kd11/matrices/mean_L40_s42.json` (110 off-diagonal cells with sycophancy_mt, 90
without), for every feature and all three probes. Cross-checked the 11-dataset column against
`audit/phase2/layer_sensitivity.csv` (store `kd11`, `dataset_set=all`), which agrees to 6 decimals.

the sharper version of the note's point: dropping the dataset helps persona features specifically,
not every feature.
