## Level 2
The row means are recomputed by my own code from the stored per-cell AUROCs, so the summary
statistic is verified. The per-cell AUROCs themselves come from the collaborator-independent
crossgen run on the lab PC and were **not** re-derived from activations (that would mean
re-training the probes); the entry is therefore Level 1 + a recomputed aggregate.

## Things the entry does not say
1. **The zero-shot claim holds only for the `contrast` direction.** For `PC1` the matched
   slice moves the other way: 0.668 → 0.625. "Zero-shot barely moves" is true of one of the
   two stored zero-shot readings and false of the other.
2. **A matched control exists in the same file and is not quoted.** `datapca` at the same
   cell reads **0.686** (matched) and 0.574 (full), so `axes` beats the matched control by
   +0.066 here. That is favourable to F-03 and should be in the entry — F-72 later criticises
   F-03 for quoting "no matched control at all", and on *this* store the control is passed.
3. **The layer caveat is the big one**, and F-72 supersedes it. Read down the same store
   (mean pooling, 21 layers), the matched-slice `axes` number runs from **0.356 at L20** to
   **0.752 at L40**; at L20 it is 0.257 *below* raw (0.613). See `audit/claims/F-72.md`.
