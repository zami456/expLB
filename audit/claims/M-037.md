## Verdict — HOLDS, but the memory note omits three qualifications F-66 recorded

   but only **3 of 21 under `last`**.

   is +0.0682 with P = 0.098 — it does **not** clear; at L20 the win count is 8 of 10, not 9.

The note's own added sentence "First positive result in the project to clear that bar" is an
editorial claim, not a measurement; it is consistent with Phase 3's finding that 276 persona-vs-raw
tests at the reported operating points clear nothing.
