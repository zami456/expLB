## The claim, stated precisely

`results/subsets/harm-pressure-knowledge-report.csv`:
**2,139 rows, 139 deceptive / 2,000 honest (6.5% positive)**.
Slices: text-matched **258** (124 deceptive / 134 honest), knowledge-matched **2,001**.
Items with a consistent belief: **1,962** (**1,841** knows / **121** not-knows).

## What I did

   with the slice rules transcribed from `eval_datasets.py::hpkr_slice`.

   `(subdataset, question text after "Consider the following question:")`, because the CSV's

## Small wording problem
"Items with a consistent belief: **1,962** (1,841 knows / 121 not-knows)" reads as if 1,962
items have a consistent belief. They do not: **1,962 is the total item count**, of which
1,841 have a consistent belief (`model_belief != -1`) and 121 do not.

The cited source (`eval_datasets.py` `load_csv_subset` + `hpkr_slice`) produces the row-level
and slice numbers but **not** the 1,962 / 1,841 / 121 item split — that comes from
`kd/scripts/build_factor_probes.py`. The source line is incomplete, not wrong.
