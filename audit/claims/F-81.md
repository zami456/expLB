   mid-network and is the layer F-91/§5e call the peak. (L60/L64 can fairly be called late; L32
   cannot.)

   ledger prints 0.855; **L20's margin is +0.1705**, the ledger prints +0.171 (it subtracted the
   already-rounded 0.803 − 0.632).

recomputation of the intervals.
