# H-012 -- the zero-shot extended axis comes "within 0.03 AUROC" of the privileged upper bound

**Source:** `src/probes/deception-detection/results/loo_L20/summary.md:95-96` (commit `6b16a46`,
2026-09-20), under the comparison table at lines 86-93.

## The claim in one sentence

Over the five shared Liars' Bench datasets the zero-shot extended persona axis at layer 40 beats
both published non-privileged probes on AUROC and balanced accuracy, and lands within 0.03 AUROC of
the Upper-Bound Probe, which is allowed in-dataset training examples.

## Level 1

```
-- layer 20 -- upper bound 0.9300, extended axis 0.8868, gap = 0.0432  (<=0.03? False)
-- layer 40 -- upper bound 0.9300, extended axis 0.8990, gap = 0.0310  (<=0.03? False)
   balacc: upper 0.89, ext 0.6939, MeanProbe 0.636, FollowUp 0.578
```

Files: `loo_L{20,40}/liars_bench_comparison_loo.csv`, rows `Upper-Bound Probe` and
`persona axis, extended (zero-shot)`. The row the claim refers to is the L40 one (the table above
the sentence is explicitly labelled "persona axis, extended, **L40** (zero-shot)"), whose stored
AUROC is **0.8990023281603701**.

**0.9300 - 0.8990023 = 0.0310.** The gap exceeds 0.03. It rounds to 0.03 at two decimals, which is
presumably how the sentence was written, but the strict claim fails by 0.001. At the layer the file
is *named* for (L20) the gap is 0.0432.

## The companion clause holds

"beats both published non-privileged probes on both metrics": at L40 the extended axis reads
balacc 0.6939 / AUROC 0.8990 against Mean Probe 0.636 / 0.682 and Follow-Up 0.578 / 0.634. True on
all four comparisons.

## The published comparator is independently verified

The Upper-Bound row is transcribed from the paper, not measured. I re-extracted **Table 8 p.56**
(pypdf page 55) of `papers/2511.16035v2 (1).pdf`, Llama-3.3-70B row:

```
   Table 8 Upper-Bound Probe    5-shared AUROC 0.930 (his CSV 0.93)   balacc 0.890 (his CSV 0.89)
      raw row AUROC  [0.74, 0.97, 1.0, 0.95, 0.99, 0.93]
      raw row balacc [0.67, 0.95, 1.0, 0.9, 0.93, 0.89]
```

His 0.930 / 0.890 are the correct 5-shared means (Table 8 prints "?" for ST and GS, so the paper's
own AVERAGE column equals the 5-shared mean here). Tables 6 and 7 also check out (0.682 / 0.636 and
0.634 / 0.578).

**Note the asymmetry the sentence does not flag:** on balanced accuracy the same comparison is
0.694 against 0.890, a gap of 0.196. "Within 0.03" is true (to rounding) of one metric only.

## Level 2

Not possible for the extended-axis figure: it is a projection of his pooled activations, which are
gone. The AUROC is however reproducible from his own per-dataset `persona_ood_ext_L40/zero_shot.csv`
(recomputed 5-dataset mean 0.8990, matching), so the aggregation step is verified.

## Verdict

**PARTLY MATCHES HIS DATA.** The comparison is real and the published comparator is correctly
transcribed, but the specific quantifier "within 0.03" is contradicted by his own CSV: the gap is
0.0310 at L40 and 0.0432 at L20. The honest wording is "within 0.031 on AUROC, but 0.20 behind on
balanced accuracy".

Confidence HIGH (both numbers read directly from his file and from the PDF).
Importance HIGH -- "nearly as good as the privileged upper bound" is exactly the sentence that
would be lifted into an abstract.
